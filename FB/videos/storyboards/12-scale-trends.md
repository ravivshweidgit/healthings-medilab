**Length:** ~40s · **Job:** After **Yes** on a Withings scale, show what the reading becomes — body composition, Trend charts, energy balance, and a light BMR one-liner.

**Id (prompt107):** `scale-trends`

**QS scale Yes/No:** use `scale-choice` (013), not this film.

## Teaching points (must land)

1. Scale reading lands in Healthings as **weight + composition** (muscle, fat), not a vanity number alone.
2. **Trend & Energy** is where composition and energy move over days.
3. Energy balance = eaten vs burned; burned includes **BMR + activity**.
4. **BMR** = resting burn — one calm line, not a lecture.
5. Scale feeds charts; charts feed coaches / clinic loop.
