# 10 — Phone health

No Withings watch? Phone health still works.
Your watch app writes steps and heart rate into Health Connect or Apple Health.
Brand does not matter — Garmin, Samsung, Apple Watch.
Healthings asks to read that store — not to replace your watch app.
Allow once. Pull to refresh — activity shows from the phone.
Scale can still be Withings or manual. This film is only about activity. Healthings.
