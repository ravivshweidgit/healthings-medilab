# 18 — The gap (Swiss / navigator)

The clinic visit ends with a report.
Clear direction. Written carefully.

Then you go home to execute it — alone.
Who supports the Tuesday meal?
A Facebook group. A search. A guess.

That is the gap.
Healthings closes it.

The clinic sets the metabolic plan.

Saturated fat and soluble fiber for cholesterol.
Added sugar when glucose is the fight.
Every meal is checked against that destination.
Scale, CGM, and labs show what's actually happening.
The clinic sees the real week — and adjusts.

Not another report that sits in a PDF.
One loop: directs, executes, measures, closes.
