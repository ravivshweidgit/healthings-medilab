# 11 — CGM pipeline

Tracking glucose? Healthings charts what your phone health store holds.
On Android, live CareSens needs a chain: CareSens, then xDrip+, then Health Connect, then Healthings.
Skip xDrip and wait on Samsung Health? Often once a day — not live.
On iPhone: share Blood Glucose to Apple Health, then Allow in Healthings.
After Allow and sync, the CGM row turns OK and the chart fills.
CSV Import helps history. Live still needs the write path. Healthings.
