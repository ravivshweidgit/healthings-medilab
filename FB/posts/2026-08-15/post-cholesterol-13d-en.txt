Documented Nutrition Protocol: Lipid Response Over 13 Days (Single-Subject Case)

Protocol summary
We documented a structured, rule-based nutrition protocol over 13 days, with laboratory lipids measured before and after the window. Meals were logged daily in Healthings. Clinical dietary rules were set with a licensed dietitian. No cholesterol-lowering medication was used during this interval.

This is a single-subject (n=1) observational case note — not a randomized trial and not a guarantee of similar results for others. Shared for transparency: labs, rules, and the actual menu.

Study window
• Start labs: 16 June 2026
• End labs: 29 June 2026
• Duration: 13 days
• Lipid-lowering medication: none in window

Primary endpoints (serum lipids, mg/dL)

• Total cholesterol: 225.6 → 173.1 (reference <200) — entered target range
• LDL: 170.0 → 118.5 (reference <100) — −51.5 mg/dL; still above target
• Triglycerides: 60 → 60 (reference <150) — already in range; unchanged
• HDL: 44 → 43 (reference ≥40) — remained in range

Chart: in-app Cholesterol trends (attached). Highlighted interval on the chart = 2nd → 3rd draw (16–29 Jun). Earlier/later draws are context only.

Intervention — dietary rules applied
1. Prefer unsaturated fats (fish, nuts, seeds, olive oil, avocado)
2. High fiber daily (psyllium + vegetables + oats; fiber target ≥ ~35 g/day)
3. Carbohydrate floor maintained (≥ ~65 g/day per rules)
4. Protein primarily from fish, poultry, and protein isolates
5. Minimize fatty cheeses and red meat during the window
6. Every meal logged; adherence checked against rules daily

Adherence method
Continuous food logging in Healthings + dietitian-aligned constraints. Loop: labs → rules → logged meals → retest.

Typical daily pattern (modal menu)
• Morning: protein powder + psyllium + oats + almonds / walnuts / pumpkin seeds
• Midday: fish or chicken breast + vegetables + olive oil + lemon
• Evening: salmon (or other fish) + vegetables + olive oil / avocado
• Often: additional protein + psyllium later in the day

Highest-recurrence foods in the 13-day log
olive oil · almonds · psyllium · pumpkin seeds · walnuts · avocado · salmon · protein powder · oats · green & cruciferous vegetables · chicken breast / tilapia

Interpretation (cautious)
In this single documented case, total cholesterol moved into the conventional <200 range and LDL fell substantially, while remaining above the <100 target. Triglycerides and HDL were already acceptable. Causality cannot be proven from n=1; the value of the record is the transparent pairing of labs with the actual menu.

Limitations
Single subject · short window · no control arm · lifestyle factors beyond diet not isolated · not generalizable · not a substitute for medical care.
