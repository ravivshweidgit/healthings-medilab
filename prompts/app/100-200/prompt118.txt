## Problem

The website has one plate collection (`lipid-protocol`) and the app linked it from
**Log Meal** — hardcoded, for **every user**:

    onPress={() => void Linking.openURL(platesUrl(lang?.code ?? 'en'))}

So a pre-diabetes patient whose clinician wrote a glycemic order was shown a
cholesterol-period food diary. That is the bug this batch fixes.

## Design (owner, 2026-08-25)

**Michal picks the collection in the clinic portal. Nothing is inferred.**

One dropdown on the Rules tab, saved on change. The app shows a link to whatever she
picked, and no link when she picked nothing. That is the whole feature.

| Source | When |
|--------|------|
| The clinic's pick, on `overlay.macros.plateCollection` | Whenever she set one |
| none | Hide the link — never default to lipid |

### Design correction (2026-08-25, same day)

The first build of this batch inferred the collection two ways: a `plate_collection` field
that Propose asked Gemini to choose from the rules prose, plus a labs fallback mapping
`computeClinicalProfile().primary` → slug. Both were removed.

The clinician already decides — she writes the order. Asking a model to re-derive her
decision from her own prose adds a way to get it wrong for no new information, and the
labs fallback added a second, disagreeing answer. What replaced ~4 levels of precedence,
a prompt block, and three lab-status reads per screen is one field she sets.

Keep this in mind before re-adding inference: **the signal is not missing, it is upstream.**
The lab-routing failure cases that motivated the fallback (a statin patient whose LDL now
reads normal; high LDL under an order that actually centers a renal cap) are both solved by
asking the clinician instead of guessing — she is the one who knows which it is.

## Design rules

- **Closed enum, validated server-side.** `PLATE_COLLECTIONS` in
  `server/src/services/plateCollections.ts` is the single source of truth and must mirror
  what exists under `website/{lang}/plates/`. Anything else normalizes to `null`, so no
  stored value can deep-link the app to a 404.
- **The pick survives everything.** It is stored independent of macro bounds and preserved
  across rules rebuilds — see "Preserve on rebuild" below. Saving rules must never silently
  clear a profile she chose.
- **Mentor-only.** The picker is hidden on `selfView`; the route asserts mentor access.
- **No auto-navigation.** Never open the browser on Save / Accept. Show a link the user
  taps. Plates are illustrations, not a prescription.
- **Content gates the picker.** A slug is only offered once its pages exist in all
  `HELP_LOCALES`. Ship the website collection first, then widen the enum.
- Plates stay on the **website** (photos, disclaimers, 10 locales). The app only routes
  locale + slug, exactly like `helpUrl()`. Do not bundle plate content in RN.
- Plates are **Log Meal examples**, not validated meal plans. They do not have to satisfy
  the patient's macro bounds and must not claim to. Keep the existing
  "Not medical advice / confirm every meal in the app" disclaimer on every collection.
- **Do not parse rules text for this.** Not with regex, and not with a model either — the
  clinician picks. (`ai-judgment-not-regex` forbids the regex; this batch forbids the
  round-trip.)

## What ships

### A. Server — registry, write path, preservation

- `server/src/services/plateCollections.ts`: `PLATE_COLLECTIONS` (v1: `lipid-protocol`)
  plus `normalizePlateCollection(raw): PlateCollection | null` (trim, lowercase,
  membership). No display labels here — those are portal `clinicLocale` copy.
- `PUT /v1/clinic/patients/:patientId/plate-collection`, body `{ collection: string|null }`
  → `setPlateCollectionForPatient()`. Deliberately **independent of macro bounds**: the
  clinic sets a profile once and later bounds edits leave it alone. An unrecognized slug
  stores as `null` rather than 400 — the app reads that as "no plates link".
- **Preserve on rebuild.** `saveMacrosForPatient()` treats `meta.plateCollection` as a
  tri-state: `undefined` keeps the current pick, `null` clears it, a slug sets it. So every
  existing bounds writer keeps the pick without knowing the field exists. The two rules
  rebuild paths rely on this — `rebuildMacrosFromRulesForPatient` omits the field, and
  `rebuildMacrosForPatientSelf` passes `overlay?.macros?.plateCollection` explicitly
  because it builds its payload directly.
- No route edits were needed for **reads**: both `GET /v1/clinic/patients/:id/overlay` and
  `POST /v1/account/macros/rebuild` return the whole `ClinicMacrosPayload`, so the field
  rides through. `macrosPayloadFromUnknown` re-normalizes stored slugs on read, so a slug
  retired from the registry stops resolving instead of being trusted forever.

### B. Clinic portal — the picker

- `plateCollectionSectionHtml()` / `wirePlateCollection()` / `savePlateCollection()` in
  `website/clinic/clinic-workspace.js`, rendered inside `renderRules()` after the editor
  fold. Labeled `<select>` copied from the `treat-field` pattern; saves on `change` (no
  second Save button); inline `#plates-status` / `#plates-error` like the rules fold.
- The portal keeps its own `PLATE_COLLECTIONS` list — it must stay in step with the server
  registry. A slug the server does not know normalizes to `null` on write, so drift
  degrades to "picked nothing", not a 404.
- Option labels resolve through `wsPlates<Slug>` (`lipid-protocol` →
  `wsPlatesLipidProtocol`), so adding a collection is one enum entry + one string per
  locale.
- New workspace i18n keys, 10 locales: `wsPlatesTitle`, `wsPlatesNone`, `wsPlatesHint`,
  `wsPlatesSaving`, `wsPlatesSaved`, `wsPlatesSaveFailed`, `wsPlatesLipidProtocol`.

### C. App — read the slug

- `ClinicMacroBoundsStore` gains `plateCollection?: string`, parsed defensively.
- `FoodLogModal` and `MacroTargetStrip` each read `store?.plateCollection` on open and
  **hide the "Example plates" link entirely when it is absent** — no lipid default.
- `MacroTargetStrip`: a calm inline row on the `active` screen — label from
  `foodLogUiCopy.examplePlates` plus `examplePlatesDisclaimer` (illustrations only, every
  meal confirmed in the app). Text link, no auto-open. It renders whenever the strip is
  active rather than only on the Accept frame, so the link is still there when My Macros is
  reopened.
  **Open taste call (owner, on device):** tighten to Accept-only if it reads as clutter.

### D. Website — second collection

- Extend `plates-locale-content.mjs` / `plates-locale-more.mjs` with the new collection in
  all `HELP_LOCALES`; regenerate with `gen-plates-locales.mjs`.
- `/{lang}/plates/index.html` lists collections with a one-line "for a Glycemic-primary
  order" style caption — no clinical claim.
- Keep the `plates-data` JSON block per page (id, slot, items, grams) so a future one-tap
  import stays possible.
- Then add the slug to **both** enums (server registry + portal list) and one label string
  per locale.

## Collection roadmap — which profiles to build

Now that a human picks, the constraint is no longer "can we route to it" but "is the page
worth building". Michal decides; this is the standing suggestion.

| Priority | Slug | Order it serves | Note |
|---|------|-----------------|------|
| shipped | `lipid-protocol` | Lipid-primary | Live, 10 locales |
| **next** | `glycemic-protocol` | Glycemic-primary — pre-diabetes → T2D | Biggest overlap with our users; CGM makes the meal→response loop visible in-app |
| 2 | `weight-protocol` | Body-composition-primary — protein-forward, satiety | Covers the largest self-serve group, who today see nothing |
| 3 | `glp1-support` | Rapid loss on GLP-1 — lean-mass protection, small volumes, protein + fiber floors | Timely; Withings muscle-mass trend is real supporting data |
| 4 | `renal-protocol` | Renal protein cap | Clinician-picked only — which is now the only way anything is picked |
| on demand | `low-carb-protocol` | Explicit low-carb / keto | |

**Pre-diabetes vs diabetes — one collection.** Do not ship a pre-diabetes-specific page or
copy that implies HbA1c banding. The clinician's order carries that distinction, and she is
the one choosing the collection.

Deferred, with the reason (do not "add anyway"):

- **DASH / hypertension** — no sodium code and no blood pressure in the snapshot, so the
  plates could not be checked against anything the app knows.
- **MASLD / fatty liver** — needs `ALT` / `AST` canonical codes first (lab catalog work).
- **Thyroid** — iodine / selenium are be-45 **markers**, i.e. a constraint layered on any
  plate, not a diet profile of its own.
- **Mediterranean** — a pattern, not an order, and it overlaps lipid. If it ever ships, it
  is the generic example on the plates index.

## Out of scope (explicit)

- One-tap "import this plate into Food Log" (the `plates-data` JSON keeps it possible)
- Checking plate macros against the patient's bounds / meters
- Any inference of the collection — from rules text, labs, or a model (see Design correction)
- Per-plate deep links from coach chat (`#yogurt-breakfast`)
- Letting the patient pick their own collection on self-serve
- Translating clinic-written rule text — still the open question in `language-policy.mdc`
- Plate photography, page layout, and Hebrew plate copy — owner + Michal
