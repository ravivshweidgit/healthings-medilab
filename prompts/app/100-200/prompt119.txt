## Problem

Michal (clinical nutritionist) could not read the meters and redrew them. Today both numbers sit
**after** the bar in one string — `MacroMeterBar.tsx` `valueText`, and the twin `MacroBar` in
`MacroTargetStrip.tsx`:

```
P  [========]  40 / 80g
```

Three defects, all in the same 118dp column:

1. **The slash is ambiguous.** Two unlike quantities, one separator, no cue which is which.
2. **Nothing aligns.** `40 / 80g`, `1772 / 1686`, `102  90–113g` are all different widths, so the
   column cannot be scanned vertically across kcal / P / C / F / Fi / C−Fi / markers / H2O.
3. **Direction rides on one glyph.** `≤` vs `≥` is a memorized convention; a real share of adults
   cannot tell them apart at 11px. A ceiling read as a goal is the worst possible misread — the
   patient chases a number they must stay under.

## Owner decisions (2026-09-01)

| Decision | Note |
|---|---|
| Adopt Michal's split — eaten before the bar, target after | Her drawing, her wording intent |
| **Words only, one mode** — `up to 100` / `above 80` / `1200–1800` | No math mode for the patient |
| **No toggle, no pref** | Rejected: a switch for how one number is phrased is a smell, doubles copy across 10 locales, and ~nobody opens it |
| Track carries direction — allowed zone + target mark | Reads pre-verbally; makes the words a confirmation, not the only signal |
| Clinic portal keeps `≤ ≥` | Reader there is a clinician (`language-policy` — `clinicLocale`, unchanged) |

## What ships

### A. Column split — eaten before bar, target after (mirrored for he/ar)

Logical order is always label · eaten · track · target. In LTR locales the row paints
left→right; in he/ar it paints with `row-reverse` and the track is `scaleX(-1)` so fill
grows right→left. An early reverse clipped the target on fat columns — safe only with the
narrow `METER_*` widths below.

| Slot (logical) | Content |
|---|---|
| Label | axis word in `appLocale` |
| Eaten | number only — carries the status color |
| Track | zone + fill + mark |
| Target | `80g`, `up to 100g`, `1200–1800` |

**Row labels are words in `appLocale`, not letter codes.** Michal (2026-09-01 phone):
`P C F Fi C-Fi` is unclear to patients. Food Log / My Macros / Quick Start meters use
`barProtein` / `barCarb` / `barFat` / `barFiber` / `barNetCarb` from `foodLogUiCopy`
(e.g. he: חלבון / פחמימות / שומן / סיבים / פח׳ נטו). Water uses `ui.water`. Units
`kcal` / `kJ` stay English (`language-policy`). Clinic portal may keep letter codes.

Drop `actual / target` everywhere it renders, including kcal, H2O and marker rows. No row keeps
the slash — a mixed strip is worse than either style alone.

**Width guard (this is the risk).** Side columns: label 56 / eaten 40 / target 78 / gap 4,
track `minWidth` 64. **The bar is the fastest signal on the row and must not pay for the
text:** shorten `bar*` catalog strings rather than widen the label. Verify at full row count
(markers + H2O visible), in de / ru / tr / he, at max font scale.

### B. Wording — one formatter, `appLocale`

| Bound | English | Hebrew |
|---|---|---|
| Ceiling | `up to 100g` | `עד 100g` |
| Floor | `above 80g` | `מעל 80g` |
| Band | `1200–1800` | `1200–1800` (`ltr`) |
| No bound (point target) | `80g` | `80g` |

Two corrections to Michal's sketch — tell her, do not silently follow it:

- **`up to`, not `under`.** Her `מתחת ל-100` is strict less-than. Our HARD ceiling **includes**
  the bound (owner: 113 allowed, 114 not), so `עד` / `up to` is the only correct reading.
- **Ranges ascend.** Her `1800 - 1200` reads as a descending pair and looks like a data bug.
  Always `1200–1800`, en-dash, `dir="ltr"` inside RTL rows.

10 locales in `foodLogUiCopy.ts`. Plain local prose, not translated English word order
(`localize-speak-like-a-person`). Units (`g`, `kcal`) stay English (`language-policy`).

Markers keep their `%` chip from prompt114 (`18` … `up to 19g` `10%`).

### C. Track zone + mark

Direction becomes graphic instead of a glyph:

| Bound | Track |
|---|---|
| Ceiling | allowed zone to the mark, dead/tinted beyond |
| Floor | muted until the mark, allowed past it |
| Band | allowed between floor and ceiling marks, dead outside |
| No bound | unchanged fill |

- Colors from `theme/tokens.ts` only. Zone must hold contrast in **both** themes
  (`dark-theme-tokens`) without competing with the status fill sitting on top of it.
- Status colors on the fill are **unchanged** — green when met / in band, red when out, axis hue
  otherwise. This is backdrop, not a new state. Michal's orange "below recommendation" legend is
  still **not** adopted (settled in `002/001` reply); do not reopen it here.

## Decisions taken while building (2026-09-01) — three refinements to the spec above

**1. A local goal keeps a plain number; only a clinic bound gets words.**
Writing every floor as `above N` produced `above 2000ml` on the water bar, which reads as a
warning about drinking too little rather than a goal to reach. Fibre without a clinic order has
the same problem. So: **clinic floor/ceiling/band → words; no clinic bound → plain `80g`.** The
zone still shows the direction in both cases, so nothing is lost. This keeps prompt119's intent
(kill the slash, phrase inequalities in words) without inventing an order the clinician did not
give.

**2. The track scale runs 25% past the target (`SCALE_HEADROOM`).**
An allowed zone is invisible if the target sits at the right edge, which is where the old
`value / target` scale put it. With headroom the mark lands near 80% and an overshoot reads as
visible distance instead of a pinned full bar. **This changes what a full bar means** — that is
the owner taste call, and it is why this needs a device, not a screenshot.

**3. Marker percents moved from a caption regex to an explicit `percentChip` prop.**
The bar used to sniff `^(\d+)%` out of the caption string to decide whether to render an inline
chip. That broke as soon as the caption was localized — Turkish writes `%30`, not `30%`. The
caller now says what it wants, and a regex left a render path.

Implementation notes worth keeping:

- `ResolvedAxisMeter.captionInfo` is the structured caption (`activityAdded` /
  `activityNotCounted` / `percentOfOrder`); the English `caption` string stays for prompt blocks
  and exports. **A screen must format from `captionInfo`** or the caption ships in English.
- The collapsed clinic summary renders **only when the log is on today**, because `clinicMeters`
  resolves for the browsed day while the collapsed header speaks for today. Showing a past day's
  order under today's energy would be a false claim.
- **he/ar chrome mirror (2026-09-01):** logical order stays label · eaten · track · target;
  `flexDirection: 'row-reverse'` puts labels on the right and targets on the left; track
  `scaleX(-1)` makes fill + zone + marks grow right→left. Other locales stay LTR. An earlier
  reverse clipped the target on fat columns — narrow `METER_*` widths are what make this safe.
  Target phrases that mix digits + `%` still use an LTR isolate (`\u2066…\u2069`).
- A percent ceiling with no `value` no longer emits `undefined% of 2000`.
- **Width pass (2026-09-01 evening):** label/eaten/target/gap tightened (`56` / `40` / `78` / `4`,
  track `minWidth` 64) so the bar gains ~25% on a phone card. Shared constants
  `METER_*` in `MacroMeterBar` keep the My Macros twin aligned. Long `bar*` strings were
  shortened in **all** locales (de-style: `Carbos`, `كربو`, `Karb.`, `Gluc. nets`, …) — never
  widen the column for one language.
