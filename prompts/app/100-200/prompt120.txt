## Problem

Michal opened the macro report on 2026-09-01 with five linked patients. Two of them
read "3 of 7 days logged". That was read as under-logging, and the kcal columns
(894, 996, 1120) supported it.

It was wrong. The clinic snapshot is **pull-based**:

- `fulfillPendingClinicSyncRequests` uploads only when a `sync_update_requests`
  row exists — i.e. after a clinician presses Refresh snapshot — and only while
  the dashboard is mounted to see it (10s poll).
- `pushSnapshotForWebView` does run on launch and foreground, but returns early
  unless the patient turned on their **own** web view. No trigger existed for a
  linked clinic.

The snapshot carries `'365d'`, so one fresh upload fills the whole week at once.
"3 of 7" therefore means the snapshot the clinic holds is four days old — not
that the patient logged three days.

Why it matters more than it looks: the report colours those days red. A dietitian
reading protein 40 g concludes her patient stopped eating protein, when the meals
were simply logged after the last upload. Discover that once and she stops
trusting the screen — the one damage a later release cannot repair.

### Keys

| Key | Travels in backup? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `healthings:clinicDailySharedDay` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, local day) | **No** | A restored value would suppress the first push on a new phone |

### Rules the implementation follows

- **Numbers only.** Plates ride on an explicit Share tap or on clinic Refresh,
  both gestures. A background push must not stream image bytes on its own —
  the same rule the web-view push already followed.
- **Local day, not UTC.** "Once a day" has to mean the patient's day.
- **Marked after the upload lands**, with a 10-minute in-memory retry window, so
  an offline morning retries the same day instead of being written off.
- **Retry window claimed only once a push is actually due** — an unlinked patient
  must not swallow the first attempt after linking.
- **Turning the toggle on pushes immediately** (`clearClinicDailyShareDay` +
  `resetDailyPushThrottle`), so the clinic does not wait for tomorrow.

### Consent basis

An approved `account_shares` row is the opt-in. Revoke stops all uploads. No separate
daily toggle — linked clinic means daily push on first open; Share is for immediate
upload + meal photos.

---

## Limits, stated honestly in the copy

There are no push notifications and no background task in this app
(no `expo-notifications`, no push tokens). So "once a day" is **the first app
open of each day**. A patient who does not open the app for four days still
shows a gap — and after this change that gap is real, which is the point: the
day count on Michal's report becomes a retention signal instead of an artifact.
