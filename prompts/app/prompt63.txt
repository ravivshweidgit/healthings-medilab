## Prompt 63 — Clinic Refresh shows newer patient My Rules

**Status: shipping 2026-07-08**  
**Builds on:** `done/prompt51.txt` (rules history / clinic overlay)

---

### Problem

Clinic **Refresh snapshot** uploaded the phone’s `user_rules`, but the Rules tab (and mentor chat) always preferred a clinic overlay once it existed — patient corrections never appeared after refresh.

---

### What ships

| Layer | Fix |
|------|-----|
| Portal `effectiveRules` | Newer `analyzedAt` wins (snapshot vs overlay); hint text explains source |
| Sync upload | If snapshot rules are newer than overlay, archive overlay → clear `rules_json` |
| Mentor chat | Same newer-wins picker for the rules prompt block |

---

### Primary files

- `website/clinic/clinic-workspace.js`
- `server/src/services/clinicOverlay.ts` (`reconcileOverlayRulesFromPatientSnapshot`)
- `server/src/services/sync.ts`
- `server/src/services/geminiClinic.ts`

---

### Verify

1. Deploy website + API restart
2. Patient edits My Rules on phone (saves with new `analyzedAt`)
3. Clinic **Refresh snapshot** → Rules tab shows patient text + “newer rules from the patient phone” hint
4. Clinic Save still adopts/overrides and pushes to phone on next open
