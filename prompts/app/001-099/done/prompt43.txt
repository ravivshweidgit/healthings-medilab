## Prompt 43 — Mentor food-science estimates (omega / nutrients)

**Status: done (2026-06-22)** — Phone-tested; user confirmed "looking good".

Builds on **prompt41** (qualitative CGM default) and **prompt23** (data-scope guardrails).

---

### Problem (user-reported, mentor_chat 2026-06-21)

Nutritionist refused omega-3/omega-6 questions despite full meal log in context:
- "מה יחס אומגה 3 אומגה 6?" → repeated "not in meal log / can't calculate"
- Even "300g salmon — what ratio?" refused until user supplied the answer
- Almost every reply opened with "רמות הגלוקוז יציבות" on non-glucose threads

Root cause: `buildDataScopeBlock` "don't invent missing metrics" was over-applied to **general nutrition knowledge**, blocking USDA-style estimates from logged food names + grams.

---

### What shipped

**Design:** Two data tiers in mentor chat:

| Tier | Source | Examples |
|------|--------|----------|
| Personal synced | USER DATA / PERIOD REVIEW only | glucose, weight, labs, logged P/C/F/Fi |
| Food-science estimate | Meal names + grams + reference tables | omega-3/6, vitamins, sodium per food, daily ω totals |

**Rules:**
- NEVER refuse a food/nutrient question
- LEAD with estimate (rough grams + ω3:ω6 ratio when asked)
- Label as estimated; brief caveat at end OK — do NOT open with "data not in app"
- NO glucose opener on omega/vitamin/nutrient threads

**Code:**
- `nutrition_knowledge` intent + `isNutritionKnowledgeQuery()` in `chatIntent.ts`
- `buildTurnHint` — nutritionist + doctor estimate-first hints
- `buildDataScopeBlock` — "don't invent" scoped to synced metrics only
- `GENERAL FOOD KNOWLEDGE` block in `buildChatSystemText`
- `buildCgmMentorRules` — skip glucose opener on food-science follow-ups
