## Prompt 75 — Food Log: Save anyway after camera keeps meal items

**Status: done (2026-07-17)** — Phone-tested Android. Commit `fb0feec`.  
**Builds on:** `done/prompt61.txt` (one-tap text meal save); `done/prompt20.txt` (photo meal flow)

### Problem

After a **camera** meal, if the Nutritionist alert appeared and the user tapped **Save anyway**, the app saved an **empty meal**. **Edit meal** then save worked.

### Root cause

First-photo auto-save (`tryAutoSaveNewMeal`) showed the issue modal when rules failed, but **did not `setItems`** with the analyzed foods (unlike the text path, which sets items before try-auto-save). **Save anyway** called `persistSave()` with `items === []`.

### What shipped

| Area | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| **Camera + alert** | On blocked auto-save, load `result.items` into the editor, clear photoSession, stay on result — Save anyway persists the real meal |
| **Guard** | Save anyway refuses empty items and shows an error instead of writing a blank entry |
