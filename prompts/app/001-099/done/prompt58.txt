### Problem

**Your setup** was read-only; labels inferred from `withingsLinked`; OAuth silently promoted routing; `activity` not wired for HC steps when watch off.

---

### What shipped

| Area | Change |
|------|--------|
| **3 toggles** | Withings scale / Withings watch / CGM — Yes/No in My Profile (`SetupToggleRow`) |
| **8 states** | `SetupToggles` ↔ `sourceConfigFromToggles()` single mapper; wizard step 2 uses same |
| **OAuth** | Removed `applyWithingsLinkToSourceConfig` — link only syncs, does not flip toggles |
| **Activity** | Watch off → HC steps on trend/energy even with Withings body (`useHcStepsForActivity`) |
| **Body routing** | `effectiveBodyScan` respects `bodyComposition === 'manual'` vs withings |
| **CGM** | Glucose chart hidden when CGM toggle off |
| **Labels** | `sourceConfigLabels` from config only (no `withingsLinked` guess) |
