## Prompt 62 — Collapsible glucose & trend (Food Log findability)

**Status: done 2026-07-08** (phone-tested)  
**Builds on:** dashboard layout; food log UX (`done/prompt61.txt`)

---

### Problem

Users struggle to find the Food Log. Tall glucose and trend charts push it far down the dashboard. Mentor strip was unclear as the AI chat entry.

---

### What shipped

| Change | Behavior |
|--------|----------|
| **Order** | Body scan → **Food Log** → Glucose → Trend & Energy |
| **Glucose** | Collapsible header; **collapsed by default**; sub shows latest mg/dL + age |
| **Trend & Energy** | One collapsible section; weight trend + ENERGY chart when expanded; sub shows weight + delta |
| **Persist** | Expand state in AsyncStorage (`dash_glucose_chart_expanded`, `dash_trend_chart_expanded`) |
| **Food Log** | Slight green border + title tint so the section reads as primary |
| **AI chat strip** | Always visible; title **AI chat** + subtitle (actions or “Ask your mentors · tap to open”) |
