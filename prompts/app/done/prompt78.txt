## Prompt 78 — HEALTHINGS.AI brand lockup

**Status: done (2026-07-18)** — commit `df6f9a9`  
**Builds on:** landing site (`prompt-be-07`), dashboard brand header, Play/TestFlight icons

### Problem

App and site still showed the old Medilab heart lockup and “Healthings Medilab” naming. Dashboard logo sat under the Android status bar (RN `SafeAreaView` ignored top inset on edge-to-edge). Tagline and export chrome did not match the metabolic OS positioning.

### Lockup decision

- Wordmark: **HEALTHINGS** [teal ECG] **.AI** — navy text; teal **only** on ECG
- App icon / mark: geometric heart on glass tile (`HealthingsMark`) — no teal ECG on heart
- Tagline: **Personalized metabolic OS with your licensed nutritionist**
- Chrome brand name: **HEALTHINGS.AI** (exports, a11y, clinic/help site)
