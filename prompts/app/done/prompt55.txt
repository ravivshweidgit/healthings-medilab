### Wizard steps

| Step | Title | Content |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Welcome | Gender, height, birthdate, language, mentor voice (he/ar) |
| 2 | Devices | Withings scale Y/N · watch Y/N · CGM Y/N |
| 3 | Weight | Manual kg OR “Link Withings on dashboard” (no OAuth in wizard) |
| 4 | Permissions | HC glucose if CGM; HC steps if **no** watch |
| 5 | Reports | Optional lab PDF + nutritionist PDF — skip OK |
| 6 | Targets | Body + daily macros — keeps saved targets for existing users |
| 7 | Food log | Tutorial + optional first meal |

---

### Manual mode (no Withings scale)

- **TREND:** weight-only chart; ≥2 weigh-in days for slope; 1 weigh-in → flat + hint  
- **ENERGY:** Mifflin BMR/day + steps→kcal + food eaten  
- **My Profile:** Log weigh-in → history + trend refresh
