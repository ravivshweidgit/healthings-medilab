## Prompt 70 — Food Log energy clarity (BMR / activity / kcal meter)

**Status: done (2026-07-16)** — Phone-tested on Samsung.  
**Builds on:** Food Log UX (`done/prompt62.txt`); energy burn from body/activity trend.  
**Commit:** `61c77b8`

### Problem

1. At **medium Dynamic Type** (iPhone), fixed `width: 56` on kcal numbers wrapped mid-value (`2,24` / `4`).
2. Users did not understand that **kcal burned = BMR + activity**, or see activity kcal separately to edit.
3. Target kcal only appeared as `eaten / 2385` on the energy line — better as a bar with P/C/F/Fi.

### What shipped

| Area | Change |
|------|--------|
| **Number layout** | `minWidth: 72`, `flexShrink: 0`, `numberOfLines={1}` on energy nums |
| **Burn parts** | `burnPartsByDay` `{ bmr, activity }` from Dashboard; total still `bmr + activity` |
| **Energy lines** | Order: **eaten** → **activity** (✎) → **burned** (caption `BMR N + activity`) → **deficit/surplus** |
| **Edit** | Correction modal = **Adjust activity calories**; delta applies to activity (and total) |
| **kcal meter** | Macro bar **kcal** above P (`669/2385`); removed `/ target` from eaten line |
