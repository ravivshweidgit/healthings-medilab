## Prompt 84 — Net-carb (C−Fi) daily target + directive macro HARD

**Status: done (2026-07-22)** — phone-tested Samsung  
**Builds on:** `done/prompt35` (fiber↔carb), Food Log C-Fi meter, My Macros / `DailyMacroTarget`, `done/prompt53` (nutrition directives)

### Problem

Nutritionists prescribe **net-carb** caps (e.g. ≤43 g) and menu summary kcal (e.g. 1690). The app stored only **C** and **Fi**; Ask macros followed ENERGY BALANCE (~2500 kcal) and derived net from C−Fi (~34) without reading the active nutritionist report.

### What shipped

| Area | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Model | `DailyMacroTarget.net_carb_g`, `aiSuggested.net_carb_g`, day snapshot field |
| Sanitize | `ensureCarbFiberNet` / `withCarbFiberNetTargets`; post-process **prefers stated kcal** (fat fills) — does not overwrite kcal from high fat |
| My Macros | Net in suggestion + edit (edit Net → `C = Net + Fi`); header includes Net |
| Food Log | C-Fi bar uses `resolveNetCarbTarget_g` |
| Ask macros | Active directive injected; **AI extracts** HARD macro summary (`extractDirectiveMacroSummary`); when directive kcal present, ENERGY BALANCE omitted and finalize forces extracted kcal/P/Fi/net |
| AI judgment | No regex parse of My Rules / directive for grams; lab markers match **canonical codes** only; cursor rule `ai-judgment-not-regex.mdc` |

### Net cap semantics

Report **פחמימות נטו: 43** = ceiling (≤43, smaller OK). Working C−Fi under 43 is fine; meter ceiling should track the prescribed net when extract applies.
