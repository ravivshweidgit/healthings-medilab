## Prompt 28 — Full CGM sample history + day-pacing macro targets

### Trigger (user reports 2026-06-09)
Export `mentor_chat_2026-06-09.html` showed mentors could cite daily CGM averages and
per-meal summaries, but failed when the user asked for granular glucose reasoning:
- "graph dropped to 40 then jumped to 100 in 15 min — compression?"
- "what is the last glucose reading and its timestamp?"
- "I thought you see full 5-min sampling history"

Nutritionist admitted: "אין לי גישה לקריאה בודדת אחרונה עם חותמת זמן... רק סיכום יומי".

Separately, midday "how am I doing today?" replies compared intake to **full-day** macro
targets (e.g. 111g protein vs 146g target at 13:48) instead of pacing by hour — making
normal mid-day progress look like failure.

User request: mentor gets **full CGM history for today and yesterday** (every ~5 min sample,
meal timestamps align with glucose timestamps), and macro guidance should match **time of day**.

---

## Fix 1 — Full per-sample CGM series (today + yesterday)

`ReviewService.ts`:
- `fetchPeriodIntraday` now returns `glucoseRaw` (merged, unfiltered) alongside `glucose`
  (warm-up + stat-filtered, used for aggregates).
- New `formatDayGlucoseSeries()` emits per day in PERIOD REVIEW when window ≤ 2 days:
  ```
  CGM ALL READINGS (N raw samples, HH:MM=mg/dL — match meal times above to these; warm-up lows NOT removed…):
    00:03=92, 00:08=90, …
    Latest reading this day: 84 mg/dL at 01:15
  ```
- RAW series intentionally keeps warm-up lows so mentor can judge compression / sensor artifact.
- Wider `/N` reviews (7+ days) keep aggregates only — full dump would blow token budget.

`GeminiService.ts`:
- `DEFAULT_SNAPSHOT_INSTRUCTION` — mentor can cite latest reading + time and analyze spikes
  from timestamped samples.
- `buildCgmMentorRules` — NEVER claim "only daily summaries" when CGM ALL READINGS is present.

---

## Fix 2 — Day-pacing macro targets

`GeminiService.ts` — new `formatDayPacingLine()` in shared profile header (coach + chat):
- Uses existing `LOCAL TIME NOW` (HH:MM refreshed each send).
- Linear guide across 07:00–23:00 eating window:
  - Reach-targets pro-rated: kcal, protein, fat ("on-pace-by-now").
  - Carbs = **full-day ceiling** (keto — never pro-rated up).
- Rule: shortfall normal for the hour is NOT failure; 0 eaten early morning is expected.

Example line:
```
DAY PACING: now 13:48, ~43% through 07:00–23:00. On-pace-by-now: ~764 kcal | P63g | F53g.
Carbs 35g FULL-DAY CEILING.
```
