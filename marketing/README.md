Scores are **ICP-specific**. Casual CGM tip-seekers flip the ranking — do not market to them first.

---

## Primary population (best fit)

**Israel-first (Hebrew) adults who already live the closed loop:**

| Must have | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| CGM (Libre / CareSens / similar) | Glucose → meal decisions, not chart-only |
| Labs that change eating (LDL / lipids, A1c, kidney) + PDF import willingness | Differentiator vs Levels / Nutrisense |
| Food log most days (photo / text OK) | Engine needs meals |
| Optional: Withings (or serious body / energy) | Burn + body → macros |
| Prefer **their own nutritionist** over ~$150–225/mo US coach membership | +clinic score **~91** vs Nutrisense **~60** |

**Job to be done:** daily macro + meal decisions from CGM + scale + food + labs — with optional clinic rules / **treatment markers** (custom caps/floors).

---

## Buyer order (GTM)

1. **Nutritionists / clinics** with lipid–metabolic patients — set rules + treatment markers; patients stay for the brief.  
2. **Those patients** (referral, QR, clinic share) — already motivated.  
3. **Solo power users** who already own CGM (+ scale) and hate Libre + Cronometer + ChatGPT glue.

---

## Anti-persona (do not lead with)

- Mass weight-loss / Noom-style behavior apps  
- “Just show my glucose” membership shoppers (Levels / Nutrisense easy path)  
- Anyone unwilling to log food or import labs  

Casual-user scores: Nutrisense / Libre / Levels win; Healthings ≈ **44**.

---

## Message pillars

| Audience | Hero line |
|----------|-----------|
| Clinic / nutritionist | Your patient. Your rules. Structured treatment macros — not a US coach roster. |
| Patient (clinic path) | Same phone file your nutritionist sees — labs, CGM, meals, targets. |
| Solo power user | Local-first closed loop. Pay as you go (~90% less than ~$200/mo). |

Avoid: “We beat Nutrisense at everything.”

---

## Pricing frame (pitch)

- **App:** pay-as-you-go AI credits (~$15–25 active month)  
- **Nutritionist:** pay the clinic when *you* choose — not a standing coach bill  
- Closed loop does the daily work; human visit is optional depth
