## TL;DR

**Wedge: clinic-first, patient-installed, clinic-sponsored AI credits — 3 Israeli private dietitians × 5 screened patients each by day 90.** D2C solo is a *support* stream (10 seats, no media spend), not the wedge.

**Channels, ranked:** (1) founder-led dietitian recruiting, (2) chair-side activation kit inside the clinic room, (3) founder-in-the-room in two Hebrew metabolic communities.

## 1. Primary persona — the clinic-referred metabolic tracker

**"Ronen", 54, Rishon LeZion. Android (Samsung). Pays for his own dietitian.**

### Who

- Type 2, not on insulin (or firmly pre-diabetic), 6 months into a GLP-1, LDL that his GP keeps circling on the printout.
- Bought a Libre OTC at the pharmacy himself — nobody reimbursed it, which is exactly why he cares what it says.
- Withings scale (or a decent scale + real interest in body composition and burn).
- Sees a private clinical dietitian every 6–8 weeks, ₪250–400 a visit (validate), and *wants* that relationship to work.
- Reads Hebrew comfortably; will tolerate English units and acronyms, not English UX.
- Lives in WhatsApp. Has abandoned 3 food apps. Still logging *something* — notes app, photos, a Google Sheet.

### Trigger (what makes today the day)

Not "I want to be healthier." It is one of three moments:

1. **A lab printout in the dietitian's hands.** LDL or A1c moved and the dietitian says "between now and next visit I need to see what you actually eat."
2. **Sensor guilt.** He is paying real money per sensor and getting a chart he can't act on. He knows the pita spiked him; he doesn't know what to do differently at 19:00.
3. **GLP-1 volume drop.** He is eating far less and someone told him protein matters now. He wants a number, in grams, for today.

### Jobs to be done

| Job | In his words |
|---|---|
| Today's numbers | "Just tell me how much protein and carbs I have left, right now." |
| Don't break the rule | "My dietitian set a limit. Stop me before the meal, not after." |
| Show the truth without a diary | "She should see my week without me writing an essay." |
| Close the loop on one meal | "Was that spike the rice or the portion?" |
| Keep the data his | "This is my medical life. It shouldn't sit in an American company's cloud." |

### Objections (and the honest answer)

| Objection | Answer we can actually make |
|---|---|
| "I'll abandon it in two weeks." | Photo a meal, done. Your dietitian sees it. That's the whole daily job. |
| "Who else sees my data?" | It lives on your phone. Nothing goes to the clinic until you tap share. |
| "I'm not weighing food." | Photo or a sentence. Grams are an estimate you can fix in one tap. |
| "How much?" | In the pilot your clinic covers the AI. After that you buy credits when you use them — quiet months cost less. |
| "My dietitian already gave me a plan." | This is the plan, metered daily, with your labs and your sensor in it. She stays your dietitian. |
| "I have an iPhone." | **Today: no live sensor on iPhone.** Scale, labs, meals and coach work; sensor is CSV import. Say this, don't fudge it. |

### Where he is

Clinic room (highest intent by an order of magnitude) · WhatsApp · Hebrew Facebook groups for T2D / sensors / keto / GLP-1 · the pharmacy sensor counter · the HMO diabetes nurse.

**He is not** on Reddit, not on Product Hunt, not reading English CGM newsletters.

## 2. Secondary persona — the private Israeli clinical dietitian

**"Michal", 41, R.D. Private clinic + two days at a diabetes center. ~70 active clients; maybe 30 who track.**

Chosen over the solo power user deliberately: she is a **repeat distributor**, she is the only one who can switch on the 91-score configuration, and she can pay. A solo power user is one retained user; Michal is a pipeline with trust pre-installed.

### Her reality

- Sessions are 45 minutes. 15 of them get wasted reconstructing "what did you eat" from memory and phone photos.
- Between visits she is blind, and clients drift, and drift looks like her failing — so she loses the follow-up revenue.
- She already asks people for food diaries. Almost nobody sustains one.
- She is not paid for admin. Any tool that adds 10 minutes per client is dead.
- She has been burned by apps that market to *her clients* over her head.

### Jobs

1. See the real week **before** the session, not screenshots.
2. Have her instruction survive contact with Tuesday night.
3. Justify the follow-up visit with something visible.
4. Zero tech support. Zero new liability.

### Objections (these are the ones that kill deals)

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| **"Am I responsible for what the AI tells my patient?"** | The rules and the limits are yours and they are enforced as hard constraints. The app doesn't invent clinical targets over your head. **Owner: get this sentence reviewed by a professional before it goes in writing.** |
| "Will you sell my client your own coach?" | There is no Healthings coach. There is no roster. We have no product that replaces you — that's the whole design. |
| "Who pays?" | You can sponsor the AI for a patient from the portal, or the patient buys their own credits. In the pilot we cover it. |
| "I'm not doing installs." | You don't. Ten minutes, in your room, with me on WhatsApp — or the QR and the one-pager. |
| "Half my clients have iPhones." | Then screen for Android when the sensor matters. Everything else works on iPhone. |
| "Is it in Hebrew?" | Portal and app, yes — including right-to-left. (Needs her eyes on it before we say it in public.) |
| "What about privacy law?" | The record is on the patient's phone; the clinic sees it only after the patient shares. Describe the mechanism, **never** assert compliance. |

### Where she is

Instagram / her own clinic page · dietitian professional groups on Facebook · Atid (עתיד) association events and continuing-ed courses · diabetes centers and endocrinology corridors · LinkedIn (weakly) · **warm introductions, which is the only channel that actually works at n=3**.

## 3. Anti-persona — refuse, or send elsewhere

| Who | Why they poison the pilot | The line we use |
|---|---|---|
| **"Just show me my glucose"** membership shopper | We score **44** for that job. Libre and Levels are genuinely better. They churn in 10 days and tell people we're complicated. | "You want the Libre app or Levels. Honestly." |
| **Mass weight-loss / Noom-shaped** | No biomarker, no logging, wants motivation. Our engine has nothing to eat. | Don't advertise near it at all. |
| **Won't log food** | The entire loop is meals. There is no passive mode and we should stop pretending one is coming. | Screening question: "Will you photo your meals most days?" No = out. |
| **iPhone user whose whole point is the sensor** | Not supported live today. Selling it is how you buy a 1-star review in Hebrew. | "Not yet on iPhone for live sensor — want the web view and a ping when it lands?" |
| **Clinic that wants a white-label / EMR integration now** | Months of work, one customer, zero learning about retention. | "Not in this quarter." |
| **Biohacker chasing flat lines** | Loud, unpaid, feature-request firehose, no clinical anchor. | Deprioritize. Don't ban — they sometimes turn into RD introductions. |

Also out of scope for the next 90 days as a *market*: the US, English-first landing pages, App Store discovery.

## 4. Wedge recommendation — clinic-first

> **Recruit 3 private Israeli dietitians. Each brings 5 screened patients. Patients install in the clinic room. The clinic sponsors the AI credits. Ship `prompt110` so the dietitian's treatment markers are live on the phone inside 30 days.**

### Why clinic-first beats D2C solo-first right now

1. **The 9-point gap is a configuration, not a feature.** Solo is 82; +clinic is 91. `Choose your real nutritionist` is literally **0** until an RD is linked. D2C-first ships our weaker product and then asks the market to grade it.
2. **The payer already exists in code.** Clinic-sponsored credits are shipped. A clinic-referred patient can run for a month without ever meeting a paywall — the cleanest retention experiment available to us, and it converts to real revenue by simply flipping enforcement on.
3. **Trust covers for polish.** UX is 76 against Nutrisense's 88, and setup is 69. A stranger from a Facebook ad judges us on that gap in the first four minutes. Michal's patient does not — she vouched, so he pushes through the sensor setup.
4. **CAC is founder time, not money.** One RD conversation can yield five users. There is no ad budget in this plan and there shouldn't be until 8-week retention exists (`EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY`: 20–50 paying users, 8-week retention, one channel).
5. **Distribution compounds.** Ten happy solo users produce ten users. One happy RD produces a second batch, then refers a colleague. That's the only shape of this business that reaches 5,000 users without a marketing budget.
6. **It answers the advisor's actual question.** "Will 500–5,000 similar users pay and stay?" A clinic funnel with a screening rule produces an honest answer. Ads produce a mixed cohort where churn tells us nothing.

### Why not D2C solo-first

Attractive because it's fast and needs nobody's permission — and it walks straight into every weakness at once: unpolished onboarding, Android-only sensor, no human accountability, and a self-selecting crowd that overlaps heavily with the anti-persona. It also burns the one asset that can't be rebuilt: the story we tell the first three RDs. "Twelve people tried it and eight quit" is not a pitch.

### Risks, and what we do about each

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **RD sales cycle is slow; 90 days yields one clinic** | Start the outreach in week 1, in parallel, warm-first. Three signed by day 30 or the channel is graded (see kill criteria). |
| **RD signs, never configures anything** | The pilot's definition of "active RD" is *rules or markers set for ≥1 patient*, not a signature. Founder walks her through the first one live. |
| **`prompt110` slips → markers stay a promise** | Then the clinic pitch degrades to "rules + the brief," which is still real (Rules 96, Sessions 95). But say it as it is, and prioritize the build. |
| **Portal Hebrew has never met a real RD** | One 30-minute QA session with a friendly RD before demo #1. `be-08` already catalogs C1–C21; extend it, don't restart it. |
| **Half the patients are on iPhone** | Screen explicitly. Track how many are lost to it — that number is the business case for `prompt56` phase 2. |
| **Founder is the entire channel** | Accept it for 90 days. Cap at 3 RDs / 15 patients so support stays survivable. |
| **One RD dropout = 33% of the pilot** | Recruit a fourth as a bench. Never let one clinic be the whole story. |

### Kill criteria (pre-committed, no re-litigating)

| Day | If this is true | Then |
|---:|---|---|
| **30** | Fewer than **2 RDs onboarded with ≥1 linked patient** | Clinic-first is not converting at founder-sales scale. Keep the one RD, move channel-1 hours to the solo power-user stream, and re-decide at day 60. |
| **45** | Fewer than **half** of linked patients logged a meal on ≥10 of the last 14 days | This is a habit/product problem, not a channel problem. **Stop recruiting.** Fix the logging loop. |
| **60** | **No RD has set a single rule or marker** for any patient | The 91 is fiction and the honest score is 82. Flip the wedge to D2C solo and rewrite the pitch around fusion + privacy + price. |
| **90** | **Zero** paid credit packs bought by anyone (clinic or patient) | Willingness to pay is unproven. No spend of any kind until a paywall test runs. |

**Upgrade signals** (any two → double down: RD self-serve onboarding + Hebrew clinic landing page + recruit 10 more RDs): an RD asks for a second batch of patients · an RD buys credits unprompted · an RD refers a colleague without being asked · ≥8 patients still logging at week 8 · a patient asks to keep going after the sponsored month ends.

## 5. Three channel bets

### #1 — Founder-led dietitian recruiting *(the wedge itself)*

- **Target:** 25 private clinical dietitians in Israel with lipid / T2D / GLP-1 caseloads. Warm introductions first; the second-degree network of a founder in Israeli health tech gets you 8–10 conversations.
- **Offer — "pilot partner, 5 patients, we cover the AI":** she keeps the client and the fee, sets the rules and the markers, gets the full week before each session, and gets a direct line to the founder who will build the missing thing that week.
- **Creative angle:** not a product demo. **Her own patient's week on screen** — one real anonymized brief: sensor curve, meals, weight trend, labs, what the rules corrected. The pitch is "this is what you'd have had before last Tuesday's session."
- **CTA:** a 20-minute video call. One link, her language, no form.
- **Cost / effort:** 8–12 founder hours a week. Media: ₪0. Gifted credit packs ~₪60–100 per patient-month (**owner decision: cap the pilot budget**).
- **Leading indicator (2–4 weeks):** 10 conversations booked → **3 RDs onboarded, ≥1 with a real linked patient and a rule set**. If demos happen but nobody links a patient, the blocker is the portal, not the pitch.

### #2 — Chair-side activation kit *(where clinic funnels die)*

An RD referral that turns into "I'll download it later" is a lost user. This channel is the ten minutes in her room.

- **Offer:** install together, on his phone, before he leaves — sensor connected, one meal logged, targets on screen, 100 credits already in the wallet.
- **Assets:** one Hebrew A5 page (what it does, what it needs, what it costs, privacy in one line) · QR to a Hebrew install page · a 90-second Hebrew screen-recording the RD can send on WhatsApp · a founder-run WhatsApp thread per patient for week 1.
- **Creative angle:** the handout leads with *"הדיאטנית שלכם רואה בדיוק מה שאתם רואים"* — the accountability is the feature, not the AI.
- **CTA:** "Photo your dinner tonight. That's it."
- **Cost / effort:** one design pass and one recording, ~6 hours, then near-zero. **Owner call: the handout and video are taste — they're yours.**
- **Leading indicator (2–4 weeks):** **≥70% of referred patients log a meal within 24h**, **≥60% still logging on day 14**. Also watch: how many drop out at the sensor step, and how many of those are iPhones.

### #3 — Founder in the room, two Hebrew communities *(solo stream + RD radar)*

Not "content marketing." No ads, no influencers, no posting schedule. Two groups — one sensor/T2D, one keto or GLP-1 — where the founder answers real questions with real screenshots, in Hebrew, as a person who does this daily.

- **Offer:** 10 seats for people who already own a sensor and a scale and will log daily. Screened by the `key-rule` question, refused otherwise — publicly refusing the wrong fit is the best advertising in a Hebrew group.
- **Creative angle:** "how I read my own sensor + labs together" walkthroughs. Show the actual dashboard, including what it can't do. Dietitians lurk in these groups; some of your best RD leads will arrive as inbound DMs from here.
- **CTA:** "Own a sensor and log most days? DM me — 10 spots."
- **Cost / effort:** 3–4 founder hours a week. ₪0 media.
- **Leading indicator (2–4 weeks):** 10 installs, **≥4 people who buy credits with their own money** (this is the only clean willingness-to-pay read in the whole plan), **≥1 inbound RD**.

### Explicitly not doing this quarter

Paid Meta/Google (unproven retention, and it delivers the anti-persona) · ASO / App Store discovery · influencers · SEO content · English landing-page work · US anything · conference booths.

## 6. Hero messages

Hebrew is written to sound like a person, not translated English — plural address matching the app catalogs (`clinicLinkCopy.ts`), local words over mid-sentence Latin, glossary English only where the repo requires it (`AI`, units).

### Clinic / dietitian

**EN:** Your patient. Your rules. And the whole week is already on your screen before they sit down.

**HE (1:1 outreach, feminine — most Israeli R.D.s):**
> המטופלת שלך ממשיכה לפי מה שקבעת גם בין הפגישות — ואת רואה את כל השבוע עוד לפני שהיא נכנסת.

**HE (catalog / plural, gender-neutral):**
> המטופלים שלכם ממשיכים לפי מה שקבעתם — וכל השבוע מחכה לכם לפני הפגישה.

*Short WhatsApp variant:* `במקום לשחזר מה הוא אכל — פשוט לראות.`

### Patient via clinic

**EN:** The same picture your dietitian sees — labs, glucose, meals, targets. One app, on your phone.

**HE:**
> הדיאטנית שלכם רואה בדיוק מה שאתם רואים — בדיקות, סוכר, ארוחות ויעדים. הכול בטלפון.

*Short:* `מצלמים ארוחה. היעדים מתעדכנים. הדיאטנית רואה.`

### Solo power user

**EN:** Sensor, scale, labs and every meal in one place — and today's targets come out of them. Pay only for the AI you actually use.

**HE:**
> חיישן הסוכר, המשקל, הבדיקות והארוחות במקום אחד — ומהם יוצאים היעדים של היום. משלמים רק על ה־AI שאתם מפעילים.

*Short:* `הנתונים נשארים בטלפון. היעדים מתעדכנים לבד.`

**Rejected drafts** (kept as a warning): *"מההרגל הזה כל השאר מתחיל לעבוד בשבילכם"* — translated English rhythm, abstract, says nothing. *"פלטפורמת ה־Metabolic OS שלכם"* — Latin mid-sentence, product-deck voice, nobody talks like that.

## 7. What NOT to say

| Banned | Why |
|---|---|
| **"Lowers your LDL"** / "מוריד לכם את ה־LDL" — or any outcome number | Clinical outcome claim. Out of scope, unproven, and the fastest way to a regulatory problem. State **what the app does**, never what the body will do. |
| **"We beat Nutrisense at everything."** | False, and the scorecard says so in writing. They win packaged sessions, polish, setup. Saying it destroys credibility with the one audience that can read a feature grid. |
| **"AI instead of a dietitian"** / "לא צריך יותר דיאטנית" | Sets fire to channel #1. The dietitian is the distribution, the differentiator, and the 9 extra points. |
| **"No logging needed — just scan and we'll tell you what to eat."** | Untrue and it recruits the anti-persona. The engine runs on meals; people who won't log will churn and say so loudly. |
| **"₪X a month for unlimited AI coaching."** | Wrong pricing frame (pay-as-you-go credits) and invents a coach SKU we deliberately don't sell. |

Two more that need care rather than a ban:

- **"Works with any CGM, any phone"** — not today. iPhone has no live sensor path (`prompt56` open). Say "Android for live sensor; iPhone for scale, labs, meals and coach."
- **"Your data is private / we're compliant"** — describe the **mechanism** ("the record is on your phone; the clinic sees it only after you tap share") and never assert a legal status. Compliance wording is a lawyer's call, not marketing's.

## 8. 90-day test plan

Three gates: **Does an RD adopt? (day 30) · Do patients stick? (day 45–60) · Does anyone pay? (day 90)**

| Week | Focus | Do | Evidence gate |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ammo | Build the demo brief from a real anonymized week. List 25 RDs, warm ones first. One-line screening rule per patient. | Demo runs end-to-end without an apology. |
| 2 | Hebrew QA + first calls | Friendly RD does a 30-min Hebrew portal pass (extend `be-08`). First 5 RD calls. `prompt110` starts. | ≥3 calls held; portal blockers listed, not discovered live. |
| 3 | Kit + close | A5 handout, QR install page, 90-second Hebrew video. Next 5 calls. | **RD #1 signed.** |
| 4 | First patients | RD #1's first 2–3 patients installed **in the room**. Community #3 opens with 10 seats. | ≥2 patients logging daily. First credit purchase from community. |
| 5 | Markers live | Ship `prompt110`; RD #1 sets a real marker on a real patient. | A marker metered on a phone, with lab provenance. |
| 6 | Second clinic | RD #2 signed and onboarded. | 2 RDs, ≥5 patients total. |
| 7 | Habit read | Instrument 14-day logging rate. Fix the worst drop-off (probably sensor setup or day-3 silence). | ≥60% logging on day 14. |
| 8 | **Gate A** | Review day-30/45 kill criteria honestly. Bench RD #4 contacted. | 2+ active RDs **or** execute the day-30 branch. |
| 9 | Depth over width | Ask RD #1 for batch two. Ask what she'd pay for. Don't add features nobody asked for. | An RD asks for more patients, unprompted. |
| 10 | RD #3 | Third clinic, ideally referred by RD #1 or #2. | ≥10 patients linked, ≥2 markers live. |
| 11 | Money | Turn on real credit purchase for the community cohort. Price conversation with each RD about sponsoring. | ≥4 self-paid packs; one clinic says yes or no clearly. |
| 12 | 8-week retention | Measure the week-4 cohort at 8 weeks. Interview every churned patient. | ≥8 patients retained at 8 weeks. |
| 13 | **Gate B** | Write the verdict against the 90-day kill criteria. Decide: scale RDs, flip to D2C, or stop and rebuild the habit loop. | The `EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY` bar: 20–50 paying users in sight, 8-week retention, one channel that works. |

### What upgrades the wedge

An RD asking for more patients · an RD paying for credits · an RD referral · patients continuing after sponsorship ends · a patient saying the marker changed a meal.

### What kills it

RDs politely enthusiastic and structurally inert (calls, no links) · patients installing and going quiet by day 4 · nobody ever setting a rule or marker · zero payment of any kind at day 90.

## Founder decisions needed (blocking)

1. **Pilot credit budget.** What's the ceiling on gifted credits for ~15 patients over 90 days? Without a number, channel #2 has no offer.
2. **Liability sentence for RDs.** The "am I responsible for what the AI says" answer must be reviewed by a professional before it exists in writing. This is the #1 deal-killer in RD conversations.
3. **Build order.** `prompt110` (phone markers) before or after the first RD signs? Recommendation: start week 2 — the clinic pitch leans on it, and `be-41` is already waiting on a deploy.
4. **iPhone.** Screen for Android on live sensor for 90 days (recommended), or pull `prompt56` phase 2 forward? Decide once, then track how many patients the choice costs.
5. **Capacity.** Are 8–12 hours a week of founder sales plus WhatsApp support for 15 patients real? If not, the pilot is 2 RDs, not 3 — better to shrink it on purpose than to fail it by attrition.
6. **Taste.** The Hebrew handout, the 90-second video, and which hero line ships are yours. No model should pick those.
