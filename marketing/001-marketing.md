## Prompt (copy below)

```
You are a marketing strategist for Healthings.AI (Medilab) — a local-first metabolic OS
(CGM + Withings/scale + food log + lab PDF → daily macros / meals), with optional
clinic nutritionist link (rules + structured treatment markers). Not a mass CGM
membership app.

## Hard constraints (do not violate)

1. Scorecard is ICP-specific. Casual “just want CGM tips” users score Healthings ~44;
   Nutrisense/Libre/Levels win that job. Do NOT recommend mass “glucose tips” GTM first.
2. We do NOT claim “we beat Nutrisense at everything.” Fair pitch only (see scorecard).
3. Pricing frame: pay-as-you-go AI credits (~$15–25 active month), ~90% less than ~$200/mo
   coach bundles. Nutritionist = pay the clinic when the user chooses — not a standing
   Healthings coach SKU.
4. Geography wedge: Israel / Hebrew-first first; US mass polish is a lose today.
5. Clinical outcome claims (e.g. “lowers LDL”) are OUT OF SCOPE — no invented medical claims.
6. Product truth: solo = strong fusion/macros; +clinic = your nutritionist + treatment
   markers (custom caps/floors HARD). Markers are clinic-set in v1.

## Inputs I will provide

- marketing/README.md (wedge draft)
- investor-pov/COMPETITOR-SCORES.md (scores, H2H, pitch)
- [optional] EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md, clinic partner notes, current channels tried

## Deliverables (be concrete, Israel-realistic)

1. **Primary persona** — one page: who, trigger, jobs, objections, where they hang out.
2. **Secondary persona** — one page (likely clinic nutritionist OR solo power user —
   pick based on fastest path to paying retention).
3. **Anti-persona** — who to refuse / deprioritize in ads and outreach.
4. **Wedge recommendation** — clinic-first vs D2C solo-first for next 90 days, with
   why, risks, and kill criteria.
5. **3 channel bets** only (not a laundry list) — ranked; for each: offer, creative
   angle, CTA, rough cost/effort, leading indicator in 2–4 weeks.
6. **Hero messages** — one line each for: clinic, patient-via-clinic, solo power user
   (EN + HE drafts; HE must sound like a person, not translated English).
7. **What NOT to say** — 5 banned lines that overclaim or attract the wrong crowd.
8. **90-day test plan** — weekly checkpoints; what evidence upgrades the wedge vs kills it.

## Style

- Direct, opinionated, fewer options with a clear #1.
- No generic “content marketing / SEO / influencers” filler unless tied to this ICP.
- Call out when you need a founder decision (taste / budget / clinic capacity).
```
