Privacy Policy

HEALTHINGS.AI is local-first: your health data stays on your phone. We do not upload your health data unless you choose to share with a linked clinic — and you can revoke that at any time. You are always in control. Last updated: July 3, 2026.

Summary

Healthings is an alpha wellness app — a personal metabolic coach for meals, glucose, and progress. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat conditions. Do not use it for emergency medical decisions. Your health data stays on your device by default. Uploading to our server happens only when you approve clinic sharing and choose to share — never automatically without your link and your action.

What stays on your device

During the alpha, the following are stored locally on your phone only:

  • CGM / blood glucose readings (via Health Connect, when you grant permission)
  • Meals, macros, and food log
  • Lab results you import
  • Personal rules, targets, and coach preferences
  • Backups you create in the app
  • Withings tokens and synced weight data (if you connect Withings)

We do not upload this health data to our server. You control exports and deletion through in-app backup tools.

Optional clinic sharing (your choice)

Sharing with a clinic is entirely optional. Nothing is uploaded until you approve a clinic in Data sharing and tap Share (or respond to a refresh request while the app is open after you have already approved that clinic). You are always in control: you choose who to link, when to share, and you can revoke access at any time.

While a link is approved and you have shared, a current snapshot sits in temporary server memory only — a handoff for that clinic, not saved in our database and not a permanent medical record. Revoke immediately purges that copy (snapshot, pending rules, and clinic workspace data for that link). The app works fully without any clinic link.

Clinic mentor chat and rule suggestions (when enabled) apply only while you keep a clinic linked. They are workspace aids for your care team — not a copy of your full history on our servers.

Optional clinic sharing may be unavailable in your region if required by law or regulator guidance. The wellness app on your phone continues to work without clinic sharing.

We do not accept patient health exports by email. Do not send health data to otp@healthings.ai — that address is for sign-in codes and account requests only.

What we collect on our server

  • Email address — for sign-in with a one-time code (no password)
  • Account metadata — login timestamps needed to operate auth
  • Optional clinic link — if you approve sharing, which clinic accounts may access your snapshot (whitelist only; no health export without your action)

API host: api.healthings.ai (HTTPS).

Third-party services you choose to use

  • Google Gemini — when you use AI coach or mentor chat, meal and health context from your device is sent to Google’s API to generate responses. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Withings — optional OAuth to sync weight; data is stored in the app on your device. See Withings privacy.
  • Health Connect — optional read access to blood glucose for on-device charts only.

Permissions

  • Health Connect — read blood glucose (wellness tracking, on-device)
  • Biometric unlock — optional fingerprint after first sign-in
  • Internet — sign-in, AI coach, optional Withings sync

Children

Healthings is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Account deletion

Alpha users can request account deletion by emailing otp@healthings.ai. Uninstalling the app removes local data from your device; server-side email records are removed on request.

Changes

We may update this policy as features evolve (e.g. optional mentor sync). Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
