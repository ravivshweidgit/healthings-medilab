Delete your account

This permanently deletes your Healthings account and everything we hold on our server. It cannot be undone.

  • Any snapshot you shared with a clinic
  • Your cloud backup, if you turned it on
  • Your clinic links — clinics lose access immediately
  • Your email address and sign-in history

Your phone is not touched. Labs, meals, weight and glucose stay in the app. To remove those too, uninstall the app afterwards — and export a backup first if you want to keep them.

We will email a confirmation code first, so nobody using this browser can delete your account without access to your email.
