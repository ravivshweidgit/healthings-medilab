Why trust it with your labs

Health data stays on your phone by default. It leaves only when you share with a linked clinic or ask the AI coach. Privacy policy.

I’m Raviv Shweid, and I built Healthings for myself first. I was diagnosed with high cholesterol, and the private program meant to fix it quoted me $3,000 — too much for a plan I would read once and file away. So I built the version that runs every day instead: home devices measure the body, my labs and my nutritionist’s plan set the limits, and one number each day says what to eat.

My own labs, 13 days apart: total cholesterol 225.6 → 173.1 mg/dL, LDL 170 → 118.5. Total is inside the general adult target of 200; LDL is not at 100 yet. No medication changed in that window — only what I ate. One person’s result, not a clinical study, and not medical advice.
