A full metabolic OS

Most health apps do one job — a glucose chart, a scale dashboard, a calorie counter, a lab PDF in your inbox. You connect the dots yourself.

Healthings Medilab is the system above that: import lab reports and your nutritionist’s plan, log meals and body trends, and let the OS learn your body over time — turning it all into today’s targets and meal decisions, with your approval before anything changes.

One system, not four apps

Charts tell you what happened. MediLab tells you what to do next.

What people use today

  • Glucose app — curve, no meal plan
  • Scale app — weight, no macro targets
  • Food app — calories, no lab context
  • Lab report — PDF sitting in email
  • Nutritionist plan — paper or PDF, not in the app
  • You — the integrator every day

Healthings Medilab

  • Lab reports imported — lipids, kidney, glucose in your targets
  • Nutritionist reports and rules — built into daily macros
  • System learns your body from food, scale, and trends over time
  • Daily macro targets and meal decisions — you confirm each change
  • Weekly review — what worked, what to change
  • Optional devices deepen the picture as you connect them

How it works

  1. Import blood lab reports and your nutritionist’s plan (PDF)
  2. Log meals and weigh in — the OS learns your body over time
  3. Review daily macro targets — confirm before they update
  4. Ask AI mentors between visits — on your plan, not generic diet chat
  5. Share a weekly brief with your nutritionist via the clinic portal

With a licensed nutritionist: they set rules and targets; you arrive at each visit with a full metabolic brief, not screenshots. On your own: same system — many users reach similar targets because the OS applies the same integrated logic to your data.

Who it’s for

People working on weight, lipids, glucose, or body composition who track between visits — and nutritionists who want that tracking in one place.

  • You log food and check in on your body regularly
  • Blood labs and nutritionist reports inform what you eat — not a folder of PDFs
  • The system learns your body and adjusts targets as your trend changes
  • You want daily decisions, not another dashboard
  • Optional scale, watch, or sensor data — the OS grows with your setup
