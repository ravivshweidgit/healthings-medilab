The apps for your phone

Healthings works on its own. Everything else here is for a device you actually own — and the list is not the same on Android and iPhone.

Showing the list for the phone you are reading this on. On a computer you see both.

Healthings

The app itself. Labs, meals, targets and trends — it runs with nothing but manual weigh-ins.

How to set it up →

Health Connect

Usually needed

Android’s own health store, and the only door Healthings uses for steps and glucose. On Android 14 and up it is already there: Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Health Connect. Older phones install it.

Already in Settings (Android 14+)

Android 13 or older: get it on Google Play

How to set it up →

Withings

Only if you have one

Scale and watch in one app. Weight, fat, muscle and BMR from the scale; steps, heart rate and workouts from the watch. Install it once, then link your Withings account in Healthings.

Get it on Google Play

How to set it up →

CareSens Air

Only if you have one

Talks to your CareSens Air sensor. On Android it keeps the readings to itself, so it needs xDrip+ below to pass them on.

Get it on Google Play

How to set it up →

xDrip+

CareSens integration

The CareSens integration Healthings uses on Android. It hands CareSens Air readings to Health Connect, where Healthings reads them.

Download the APK

How to set it up →

Samsung Health

Only if you have one

Only matters as a step source. It writes steps into Health Connect and Healthings reads them there — but you have to switch that on: Samsung Health → Settings → Health Connect. Otherwise the steps never leave Samsung.

Get it on Google Play

How to set it up →

Apple Health

Usually needed

Nothing to install. It is where CareSens Air glucose and your phone’s step count meet Healthings — you grant permission the first time and that is it.

Withings

Scale and watch in one app. Weight, fat, muscle and BMR from the scale; steps, heart rate and workouts from the watch. Install it once, then link your Withings account in Healthings.

Get it on the App Store

CareSens Air

Only if you have one

Talks to your CareSens Air sensor and shares glucose straight to Apple Health, where Healthings reads it. No bridge app on iPhone.

Get it on the App Store

How to set it up →

Less to install on iPhone

No xDrip+ and no Samsung Health here — CareSens Air talks to Apple Health by itself, and the phone counts your steps.

Not sure? Install Healthings and nothing else. Add a device app the day you own the device.
