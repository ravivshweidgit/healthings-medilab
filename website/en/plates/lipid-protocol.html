Example plates

A logged day rhythm from a lipid window

Four repeatable plates from a real food log. Rough amounts so you can type the same plate into Log Meal.

Not medical advice. This is one person’s logged food during a documented lab window — not a promise that these plates lower cholesterol. Your clinician sets My Rules and treatment markers; confirm every meal in the app before it saves.

Breakfast

Yogurt with bran, seeds, nuts & blueberries

Morning bowl — yogurt, fiber mix, and blueberries. Add 100 ml water so chia, bran, and psyllium can soak it up.

  • Yogurt 2%150 g
  • Water100 ml
  • Blueberries40 g
  • Oat bran1 tbsp (~17 g)
  • Psyllium1 tbsp (~5 g)
  • Chia seeds1 tbsp (~10 g)
  • Pumpkin seeds1 tbsp (~8 g)
  • Walnuts3 halves (~5 g)
  • Almonds4 (~6 g)

Chia, bran, and psyllium drink the water. In the app: Log Meal → text, e.g. “yogurt 2% 150g, water 100ml, blueberries 40g, oat bran 1 tbsp, psyllium 1 tbsp, chia 1 tbsp, pumpkin seeds 1 tbsp, 3 walnut halves, 4 almonds”.

Lunch

White fish (or chicken / tofu), lentils & cucumber

Midday protein + legumes + raw veg + olive oil. Swap white fish for chicken breast or tofu — no avocado here.

  • White fish / chicken / tofu150 g
  • Cooked lentils5 tbsp
  • Cucumber2 tbsp
  • Olive oil2 tbsp

In the app: Log Meal → text, e.g. “grilled white fish 150g, lentils 5 tbsp, cucumber 2 tbsp, olive oil 2 tbsp” — or chicken / tofu instead of fish.

Evening

Salmon sashimi, avocado, ginger, teriyaki & Thai salad

Evening plate from the log: sashimi + avocado, with a finely chopped Thai salad (sprouts, pepper, cucumber, onion, lettuce, mint, basil), ginger, and teriyaki.

  • Salmon sashimi~100 g
  • Avocado (½)~90–100 g
  • Pickled ginger~10 g
  • Teriyaki sauce~10–15 g
  • Sprouts50 g
  • Bell pepper40 g
  • Cucumber30 g
  • Onion20 g
  • Lettuce30 g
  • Mintfresh
  • Basilfresh

Salad veg is all finely chopped. In the app: Log Meal → text, e.g. “salmon sashimi 100g, half avocado 100g, pickled ginger 10g, teriyaki 15g, sprouts 50g, bell pepper 40g, cucumber 30g, onion 20g, lettuce 30g, mint, basil — all finely chopped”.
