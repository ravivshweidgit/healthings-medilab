Example plates

Four small-volume plates for GLP-1 support

Gentle textures, modest portions, protein and fiber still present — for Log Meal. Concept draft; your clinician sets exact floors.

Not medical advice. Support illustrations only — not dosing or medication guidance. Confirm every meal in the app; lean-mass protection is set in My Rules.

Breakfast

Soft scrambled eggs & small toast

Easy to eat when appetite is low — protein first, small starch.

  • Soft scrambled eggs2 eggs (~120 g)
  • White toast1 slice (~30 g)

In the app: Log Meal → text, e.g. “soft scrambled eggs 2, white toast 1 slice”.

Lunch

White fish & mashed cauliflower

Half a usual lunch volume — protein on a soft base.

  • White fish, baked80 g
  • Mashed cauliflower100 g
  • Olive oil1 tbsp

In the app: Log Meal → text, e.g. “white fish baked 80g, mashed cauliflower 100g, olive oil 1 tbsp”.

Evening

Chicken soup with soft vegetables

Warm, sippable dinner when a full plate feels like too much.

  • Chicken soup250 ml
  • Soft vegetables in soup150 g
  • Chicken pieces120 g

In the app: Log Meal → text, e.g. “chicken soup 250ml, soft vegetables 150g, chicken 120g”.

Snack

Greek yogurt (small bowl)

Protein anchor you can finish — stop at satiety, not the bowl size.

  • Greek yogurt 0%100 g
  • Pumpkin seeds1 tbsp (~8 g)

In the app: Log Meal → text, e.g. “Greek yogurt 0% 100g, pumpkin seeds 1 tbsp”.
