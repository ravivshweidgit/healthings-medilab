Quick Start

Learn your body. Reach your targets.

Healthings is a wellness app with a professional working method: live body insight for you, clear feedback for your nutritionist, and a closed loop that optimizes the path to your targets.

Learns your body. Teaches you.

As devices and logs come in, you see live charts — weight, composition, activity, heart rate, glucose when connected — updating as your days unfold. The model builds a picture of how your body responds, then explains the results in plain language and coaches you under My Rules.

The point is not a bigger spreadsheet. It is understanding: what moved, why it may have moved, and what to do next on the way to your targets.

Body feedback for your nutritionist

When you share a clinic snapshot, your nutritionist receives structured body and lifestyle feedback — not a scavenger hunt through screenshots. That visibility lets them refine My Rules with less guesswork. You keep living the plan; they keep sharpening it. That is the optimized path to target success: patient insight + professional judgment, updated in a continuous cycle.

The closed loop (methodology breakthrough)

  1. Intent — A licensed nutritionist (or clinic) writes My Rules.
  2. Life + live data — Meals, body, activity, labs; charts as they happen.
  3. Learn & teach — Healthings personalizes to your patterns and explains results.
  4. Feedback — Shared snapshots give your nutritionist a clear body picture.
  5. Refine — Rules tighten. Targets get closer. The loop closes for both of you.

Most wellness tools stop at tracking. Most clinic workflows stop at the visit. Healthings is how care runs between appointments — professional-grade method in a wellness category.

Wellness category. Professional standard.

We stay in wellness on purpose: Healthings does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or replace your clinician. Explanations are coaching under My Rules — not medical conclusions. The ambition is methodological: high-end practice between visits, with medical decisions where they belong.

Not medical care

Healthings is a wellness execution layer. Emergency and medical decisions stay with licensed professionals. The app applies the rules they (or you) put in My Rules; it is not independent medical practice.

Your data

  • Metrics, meals, and rules stay on your device unless you choose to share with a clinic.
  • Withings / phone health links only read what you already allow those apps to store.
