Example — any Withings scale on your account

Quick Start

Do you have a Withings body scale?

Answer Yes if you weigh yourself on a Withings scale that syncs to the Withings app. Healthings reads weight (and fat/muscle when available) from your Withings account — not over Bluetooth.

Answer Yes if…

  • You own a Withings scale (Body, Body+, Body Comp, Body Scan, or similar).
  • It already shows weigh-ins in the official Withings app.

Answer No if…

  • You use a bathroom scale from another brand, or no scale.
  • You will type weight in Healthings — see manual weight & body fat.

Next steps after Yes

Quick Start will ask you to Link Withings (same account as the Withings app). See which products work and how linking works.
