Coach & meals language

This setting controls the language of the AI coach, meal names, and exported reports — not the buttons and menus (those stay in English).

What changes

  • Chat replies and coach cards
  • Meal titles in your food log
  • Clinical / visit report text you export

What stays English

Screen titles, settings labels, errors, and clinic link chrome stay English for now.

Mentor voice gender

If you pick Hebrew or Arabic, you will also see Mentor voice gender — that only changes how the coach addresses you (masculine/feminine forms). See mentor voice gender.
