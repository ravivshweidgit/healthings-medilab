Activity setup

Steps & heart rate without a Withings watch

In Healthings, Withings watch = No means we read steps and heart rate from your phone’s health app — Health Connect on Android, Apple Health on iPhone. The watch brand does not matter. You get data into that store; Healthings only reads.

The rule

  • Withings watch Yes — activity and HR from Withings cloud (link in the app).
  • Withings watch No — activity and HR from Health Connect / Apple Health.

In the app: My Profile → Your setup → turn Withings watch No → tap Allow access. Everyday sync covers recent days; tap Deep sync (~31 days) when you want more history — same idea as Withings Normal / Deep.

Do not share Withings into Health Connect / Apple Health

Healthings talks to Withings over the cloud API only. We do not recommend turning on Withings → Health Connect or Withings → Apple Health sharing. That can double-count steps with Samsung Health, Garmin, or the phone, and confuses Watch Off activity. Prefer: Withings app sharing off; phone vendor (Samsung / Garmin / …) sharing on into the health store.

Android — fill Health Connect

Pick whatever you use. Examples:

  • Garmin — Garmin Connect → Settings → Health Connect → share activities, steps, calories, heart rate.
  • Samsung / Galaxy Watch — Samsung Health → Settings → Health Connect → allow steps, workouts, heart rate.
  • Pixel / Fitbit / others — open the companion app and enable sharing to Health Connect (wording varies).
  • Phone only — many phones write steps into Health Connect with no watch.

Then in Health Connect → App permissions → Healthings → allow Steps, Exercise, Active calories, and Heart rate. Pull to refresh in Healthings.

iPhone — fill Apple Health

  • Apple Watch — usually already writes steps and heart rate to Apple Health.
  • Other watches — use the maker’s iPhone app and turn on sharing to Apple Health for steps / heart rate / workouts when available.
  • iPhone only — Motion & Fitness can contribute steps; confirm in the Health app under Browse → Activity.

Then Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Healthings → allow Steps and Heart Rate (and Active Energy if listed). Sync in Healthings.

If activity stays at zero

  1. Confirm the source app shows steps/HR today.
  2. Confirm Health Connect / Apple Health shows the same data.
  3. Confirm Healthings has Allow access (or Troubleshoot on Android).
  4. Pull to refresh the dashboard.
