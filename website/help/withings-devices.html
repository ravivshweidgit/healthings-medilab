Compatibility

Which Withings products work?

Healthings does not pair to a watch or scale over Bluetooth. It reads your Withings cloud account after you tap Link Withings once in the app.

The short answer

  • Any Withings scale that writes weight to your Withings account works (Body, Body+, Body Comp, Body Scan, and similar).
  • Any Withings watch or band that writes activity and heart rate to the cloud works (ScanWatch, Steel HR, Pulse, and similar).
  • Product photos in setup are examples — you do not need that exact model.

What we read

  • Scale — weight; fat, muscle, visceral fat, and BMR when your scale sends them.
  • Watch / band — steps and activity calories, heart rate, workouts.
  • Body Scan–class scales — best composition detail when those measures exist in the cloud.

What you must do

  1. Pair the device in the official Withings app and confirm data appears there.
  2. In Healthings Quick Start (or My Profile), answer scale / watch Yes as needed.
  3. Tap Link Withings and sign in with the same Withings account.
  4. Pull to refresh on the dashboard after walks or weigh-ins.

If the device is only in the Withings app and you never Link in Healthings, Healthings will not see the data.

No Withings watch?

Set Withings watch to No. Steps and heart rate come from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone) — Garmin, Apple Watch, Samsung, and others that write there. See steps & heart rate (phone health).

Do not share Withings into phone health

Prefer Withings → Health Connect / Apple Health sharing off. Healthings already uses the Withings cloud API. Sharing Withings into the phone health store can double-count steps when Watch is Off.
