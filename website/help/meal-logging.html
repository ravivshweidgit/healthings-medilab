Quick Start

How to log meals

Logging food is how Healthings tracks energy in vs out and coaches your day. You can start now or finish setup and log later.

Ways to log

  1. Tap + on the metabolic chart to open the food log.
  2. Photo — snap your plate; AI lists items; you approve.
  3. Text — describe your meal; AI parses macros.
  4. Coach chat can suggest what to log — save via the food log.

You can edit or delete meals anytime. Pull to refresh after walks so burned calories stay up to date.
