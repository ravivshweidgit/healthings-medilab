Alpha matrix

Devices & platform notes

What works where during the alpha. The app UI is the same; data sources differ by phone and toggles.

Recommended alpha combos

  • Withings watch only (Android or iPhone) — best supported path for loaner ScanWatch testers.
  • Withings scale + watch — full body composition + activity (Android or iPhone).
  • Android + CGM — live glucose via Health Connect (Libre / CareSens / xDrip).
  • iPhone + CareSens Air — live glucose via Apple Health (CareSens Air → share with Health → Healthings reads Blood Glucose).
  • Android + Garmin watch — activity via Health Connect (Garmin Connect sync).

CGM (glucose)

  • Android: turn CGM → Yes in Your setup; grant Health Connect blood glucose permission; companion app must write to Health Connect (e.g. xDrip+).
  • iPhone: install CareSens Air, enable sharing to Apple Health, turn CGM → Yes in Healthings, allow Blood Glucose when prompted, then Sync. CSV Import remains available as a backup.

Activity & heart rate

  • Withings watch Yes — Withings cloud on Android and iPhone.
  • Withings watch No — phone health store (Health Connect / Apple Health). Any brand that writes there works — see steps & heart rate guide.

Body weight & fat

  • Withings scale: automatic from Withings cloud.
  • No scale: manual log in My Profile — see manual body guide.

Clinic portal

If you linked a nutritionist clinic, they see a read-only snapshot of your phone data. Open the app (or pull to refresh) after big changes so they get an updated snapshot.

Same help for everyone

Quick Start, food log, coach, and My Rules work the same on Android and iPhone. Only health sources differ — not the screens.
