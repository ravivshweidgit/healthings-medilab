Setup

What connects where

The app screens are the same on Android and iPhone. Only where data comes from changes — based on your Yes/No answers in Quick Start (or My Profile → Your setup).

Pick your path

  • Withings scale — weight and body composition from your Withings account after you Link Withings. See which products work.
  • Withings watch — activity and heart rate from Withings cloud. See how to link.
  • No Withings watch — steps and heart rate from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone). See phone health guide.
  • CGM (glucose) — live chart from Health Connect or Apple Health. See the focused guide: CGM on Android vs iPhone.
  • No scale — enter weight manually. See manual body guide.

Clinic

If you linked a nutritionist clinic, they see a read-only snapshot of your phone data. Open the app (or pull to refresh) after big changes so they get an updated snapshot.
