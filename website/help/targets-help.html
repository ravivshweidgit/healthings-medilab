Quick Start

Your body & macro targets

Healthings suggests a body direction (weight / fat / muscle) and daily macros (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber) from your profile and any rules you already saved.

What you see

  • Body target — where to aim over the next weeks.
  • Daily macros — energy and P/C/F (and fiber when set).

Buttons

  • Next — save these targets and continue.
  • Regenerate with AI — ask for a fresh suggestion (overwrites the draft on this screen).
  • Retry — if generation failed (network or missing weight).

You can edit targets later in My Profile. My Rules and clinic guidance can refine them over time.
