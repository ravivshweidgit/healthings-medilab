Quick Start

Do you have a Withings watch or band?

This answer chooses where activity and heart rate come from — Withings cloud, or your phone’s health app.

Answer Yes if…

  • You wear a Withings ScanWatch, Steel HR, Pulse, or similar that syncs to the Withings app.
  • You want steps, workouts, and HR from Withings cloud after you Link Withings.

Details: link & daily refresh · which products work

Answer No if…

  • You use Apple Watch, Garmin, Samsung, phone steps only, or no watch.
  • Healthings will read steps and heart rate from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone).

Details: steps & heart rate without a Withings watch

Prefer not to share Withings into Health Connect / Apple Health — Healthings already uses the Withings cloud API when you answer Yes.
