Mentor voice gender

This only affects how the AI coach / mentors sound and address you in Hebrew or Arabic (and similar gendered languages). It does not change your profile gender or medical settings.

When you see this

It appears when your Coach & meals language is Hebrew or Arabic, because those languages often use different word forms for male vs female address.

What to choose

  • Male — mentor replies use masculine forms of address.
  • Female — mentor replies use feminine forms of address.

Pick the voice you prefer to hear from the coach. You can change it later in My Profile with language.
