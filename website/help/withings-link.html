Quick Start

Link your Withings account

One sign-in connects Healthings to Withings cloud for your scale and/or watch. Pair devices in the Withings app first — Healthings does not pair Bluetooth hardware itself.

How to link

  1. Tap Link Withings in Quick Start (or later in My Profile).
  2. Sign in with the same Withings account that owns your scale/watch.
  3. Allow Healthings to read metrics and activity.
  4. Return to the app — you should see “Withings connected”.

Skip for now?

You can tap Next and link later in My Profile. Until you link, scale/watch data will not appear. Targets may use a temporary weight estimate.
