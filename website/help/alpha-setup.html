Quick Start — watch only

For testers with a Withings ScanWatch (no Withings scale). Same steps on Android and iPhone.

1. Install & sign in

  • Android: Google Play internal test link.
  • iPhone: TestFlight link (install Apple’s TestFlight app first).
  • Open Healthings → sign in with email → enter the one-time code.

2. Quick Start toggles

In My Profile → Your setup (or the first-run wizard):

  • Withings scale → No
  • Withings watch → Yes
  • CGM → only if you use glucose on this phone (see devices)

3. Enter height, weight, age

Without a scale, the app uses your manual weight for BMR and trends. Log weight when you weigh yourself (gym, home scale, clinic).

Optional: set body fat % once (from a past DEXA, InBody, or estimate). See manual body guide.

4. Link Withings

  1. Tap Link Withings in My Profile or the wizard.
  2. Sign in with your Withings account (create one if needed).
  3. Allow the app to read activity, heart rate, and workouts from Withings cloud.

The watch syncs to Withings cloud first — the app reads from Withings, not directly from the watch Bluetooth.

5. Daily habit

  • Log meals in Food log.
  • After a walk, open the dashboard and pull down to refresh — calories burned and HR should update.
  • Chat with the coach / nutritionist mentors as needed.

Handing off a loaner watch

When your 2–4 week test ends:

  1. In the app: disconnect Withings (if available) or sign out.
  2. Factory-reset the watch per Withings instructions.
  3. Next tester links their own Withings account.
