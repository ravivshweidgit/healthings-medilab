Quick Start

Starting weight

We need a starting weight so energy balance, burned calories, and AI targets make sense from day one.

If you have a Withings scale

  • After Link Withings, cloud weight may fill in automatically — tap Next if it looks right.
  • Or enter today’s weight once, then let the scale update later.
  • You can skip and pull weight from Withings later (targets use a temporary estimate until then).

If you do not have a Withings scale

Enter your current weight now. You can update it anytime in My Profile — see manual weight & body fat.
