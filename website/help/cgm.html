Glucose

CGM on Android vs iPhone

A CGM (continuous glucose monitor) sends live glucose into your phone’s health app. Healthings only reads that data — it does not talk to the sensor over Bluetooth.

In Quick Start

Answer Yes to “Do you track glucose with a CGM?” Then allow the health permissions when the app asks. You can change this later in My Profile → Your setup.

Android

  1. Your CGM companion app (or a bridge like xDrip+) must write blood glucose to Health Connect.
  2. In Healthings: CGM = Yes.
  3. Allow Healthings to read Blood glucose in Health Connect.
  4. Open the dashboard and pull to refresh — the glucose chart should fill.

Common path: Libre / CareSens (or similar) → companion or xDrip+ → Health Connect → Healthings.

iPhone

  1. Install your CGM app (for example CareSens Air) and turn on sharing to Apple Health (Blood Glucose).
  2. In Healthings: CGM = Yes.
  3. Allow Healthings to read Blood Glucose when Apple Health asks (or later: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Healthings).
  4. Open the dashboard and Sync / pull to refresh.

If live sharing is not available yet, use CSV Import from My Profile as a backup.

No CGM?

Answer No in Quick Start. You can still import lab PDFs and use the coach without a live glucose chart.
