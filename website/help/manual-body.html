My Profile · Body

Manual weight & body fat

When you chose Withings scale → No, the app estimates BMR from your profile and any weigh-ins you log.

What to enter

  • Weight (kg) — log whenever you weigh yourself. This updates your trend and BMR.
  • Body fat — enter as Fat %, Fat kg, or Muscle kg (pick one mode). You only need one number; the app derives the rest.

What is calculated (not typed)

  • Muscle mass (kg) — from weight and fat % (lean-mass model).
  • Fat mass (kg) — weight × fat %.
  • BMR — Mifflin–St Jeor from weight, height, age, and sex.

If you enter muscle kg, the app back-calculates fat %. Fat + muscle should be close to your total weight (a small gap is normal — bone and water are not modeled).

If you skip body fat

The app uses a default estimate from your profile (shown as “fat % estimated”). For better macro and coach context, enter fat % once from any source you trust.

Withings watch still helps

Watch-only users get activity, heart rate, and workouts from the watch via Withings cloud. Manual body logging only replaces the scale — not the watch.

Food log “kcal burned”

kcal burned ≈ BMR + activity for the day. BMR comes from manual body; activity comes from your Withings watch after sync.
