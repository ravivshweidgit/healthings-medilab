About you

Gender, height, and birth date help estimate resting energy (BMR), BMI, and sensible starting targets.

Why we ask

  • Height + weight → BMI and calorie context.
  • Gender + age → better resting-energy estimates when you have no scale BMR yet.
  • You must be at least 13 years old to use the app.

You can edit these later in My Profile if anything was wrong.
