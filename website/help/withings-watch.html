Android & iPhone

Link your Withings watch

Healthings reads Withings cloud — same on Android and iPhone. Your watch must sync to the Withings app first.

Before linking

  1. Pair the watch with the official Withings app on your phone.
  2. Confirm walks and heart rate appear in Withings (wait a few minutes after exercise).
  3. In Healthings: Your setup → Withings watch → Yes.

Link in Healthings

  1. My Profile → Link Withings.
  2. Sign in with the same Withings account as the watch.
  3. Return to the dashboard when the browser closes.

After a walk or workout

  • Open the Healthings dashboard.
  • Pull down to refresh (syncs Withings cloud → app).
  • Check: heart-rate line on the chart, workout bars, Food log → kcal burned increased.

Withings cloud can lag 2–5 minutes after you stop moving. Refresh again if numbers look stale.

What the watch provides (no scale)

  • Heart rate (24/7 and during sessions)
  • Steps and active calories
  • Timed workouts (walk, run, etc.)

It does not replace a body-composition scale — use manual weight & fat % for that.

Troubleshooting

  • Empty chart — re-link Withings; pull to refresh; confirm data in Withings app.
  • Wrong account — unlink and link the account that owns the watch.
  • Loaner watch — each tester needs their own Withings login for the test period.
