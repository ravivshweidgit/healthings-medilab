Four plates for a body-composition order

Protein-forward meals with vegetables for volume — rough amounts for Log Meal. Concept draft for clinician review.

Not medical advice. Illustrations for a weight / body-composition order. Your clinician sets kcal and protein floors in My Rules; confirm every meal in the app.

Breakfast

Eggs, whole-grain toast & avocado

Protein and fat at breakfast — toast portion stays modest.

  • Eggs2 eggs (~120 g)
  • Whole-grain toast1 slice (~30 g)
  • Avocado (¼)½ (~70 g)

In the app: Öğün kaydet → text, e.g. “2 eggs, whole-grain toast 1 slice, avocado quarter 70g”.
