Platos de ejemplo

Four plates for an explicit low-carb order

Only when the clinician wrote low-carb or keto in the order — rough amounts for Log Meal. Concept draft; net carb cap lives in My Rules.

Not medical advice. For an explicit low-carb order only — never inferred from labs. Confirm every meal in the app before it saves.

Breakfast

Eggs, cheese & avocado

No toast or fruit at breakfast — fat and protein carry the meal.

  • Eggs2 eggs (~120 g)
  • Hard cheese30 g
  • Avocado (½)~90–100 g

In the app: Registrar comida → text, e.g. “2 eggs, hard cheese 30g, half avocado 100g”.

Lunch

Caesar salad with chicken (no croutons)

Big salad, full dressing — croutons stay out.

  • Grilled chicken120 g
  • Romaine & caesar salad200 g
  • Parmesan30 g
  • Olive oil1 cda

In the app: Registrar comida → text, e.g. “grilled chicken 120g, caesar salad 200g no croutons, parmesan 30g, olive oil 1 tbsp”.

Evening

Steak, asparagus & butter

Protein and green veg — no potato or rice on the side.

  • Lean steak150 g
  • Asparagus200 g
  • Butter1 cda

In the app: Registrar comida → text, e.g. “lean steak 150g, asparagus 200g, butter 1 tbsp”.

Snack

Nuts & cheese

Small handful — counted, not open bowl.

  • Mixed nuts20 g
  • Cheese cubes30 g

In the app: Registrar comida → text, e.g. “mixed nuts 20g, cheese cubes 30g”.
