Platos de ejemplo

Four plates for a renal protein cap

Smaller protein portions with starches and vegetables filling the plate — for Log Meal. Concept draft; the cap in My Rules is the law.

Not medical advice. Protein-controlled illustrations only. The renal cap comes from your clinician and labs — never from these example amounts.

Breakfast

Egg-white omelette & white bread

Most breakfast protein from whites; one small starch slice.

  • Egg-white omelette3 whites (~100 g)
  • White bread1 slice (~25 g)
  • Jam1 cda

In the app: Registrar comida → text, e.g. “egg-white omelette 3 whites, white bread 1 slice, jam 1 tbsp”.
