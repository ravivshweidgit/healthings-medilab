## 1. How ownership % works

Each person’s **contribution** is:

**Contribution = Cash you put in + (Hours you work × R)**

Your **share %** =

**Your contribution ÷ everyone’s contributions × 100%**

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Cash** | Money you fund for the product (or company bills you pay) |
| **Hours** | Agreed work time (product, clinical, ops, field testing) |
| **R** | One shared \$/hour for **everyone** (see below) |

---

## 2. Equal-effort rule (important)

We do **not** use each person’s private salary or fee for their own hours.

**R = the highest professional hourly rate among people in the project.**

Today that is the nutritionist rate: **\$133 / hour** (assuming a 1-hour session; we can adjust if sessions are shorter).

So if Raviv’s day-job rate is lower (~\$40/h) and Michal’s is \$133/h, **both** still get **\$133 per agreed Hour** inside Healthings. Dedication is valued equally — marketplace status does not create an advantage.

If someone later joins with a **higher** rate, **R rises for everyone** and all sweat is recomputed. Same rule for all.

## 5. How it will work as you contribute

1. Agree what counts as your **Cash** and your **Hours** (and that R = max rate, currently \$133/h).  
2. Add your numbers to the table → recompute every share %.  
3. Agree **vesting** for time-based share (typical: multi-year, with a cliff) so % tracks ongoing contribution.  
4. Sign real documents (advisor / founder / SAFE) — this note only explains the **math**.

**Example:** Raviv at ~\$81k contribution. Someone adds **\$20k cash only** → they get roughly **20%**, Raviv ~**80%**. If they also work **100 hours**, those hours × \$133 add ~\$13k more to their contribution and their % rises accordingly.
