Pay as you go · not ~$200/mo
Nutrisense often means ~$150–225 every month (sensors + packaged nutritionist). Healthings: buy AI credits when you use them (~$15–25 busy month) — see a nutritionist when you decide, pay the clinic then. You don’t need that standing coach bill: the closed loop (CGM + scale + food + labs + activity → macros) runs day to day.

Strong pitch
For Libre + Withings + labs users who log food and training daily, Healthings.AI scores 82 solo vs Nutrisense 60. We win local-first privacy, My Rules → real-time correction, fusion, fused charts, AI food log, fluid Help, and pay-as-you-go. They win packaged nutritionist sessions + meal-score polish. Healthings + clinic (~91) — your nutritionist sets structured treatment macros and walks in with the full brief; you still don’t pay ~$200 every month.

One sentence: Nutrisense = ~$200/mo cloud sessions with their nutritionists. Healthings = local-first closed loop + your nutritionist’s custom macros when you want — not theirs.
