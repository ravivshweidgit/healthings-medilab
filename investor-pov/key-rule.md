# Key rule — ICP & channels

**For:** GTM, clinic conversations, community beta  
**Date:** June 2026  
**Related:** [Nutricence-medilab.md](./Nutricence-medilab.md) · [EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md](./EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md)

---

## The rule (one line)

> Enroll only people the nutritionist **or you** judge as **metabolic + motivated to track**.

Not every dieter. Not every nutritionist client. **This subset.**

---

## Flow

```
        metabolic + motivated to track
                    │
        ┌───────────┴───────────┐
        ▼                       ▼
  Community / informal      Clinic / RD
  (groups, friends,          (formal or casual
   open beta)                 RD recommendation)
        │                       │
        └───────────┬───────────┘
                    ▼
              MediLab user
```

**Both channels are valid. No conflict.** Same filter, different front door.

---

## What “metabolic + motivated to track” means

### Metabolic (health goal fits the product)

At least one of:

- **Lipids** — LDL, cholesterol, triglycerides  
- **Glucose** — pre-diabetes, type 2 lifestyle, CGM user  
- **Weight / body comp** — scale-driven goals with clinical context  
- **Labs matter** — recent or planned blood work informs eating  

Often **2+ together** (e.g. LDL + weight + Libre).

### Motivated to track (behavior fits the product)

- Will **log food** between visits or daily (photo AI OK)  
- Has or will use **CGM and/or smart scale** (Withings ideal)  
- Will **open the app** as a habit — not one-off curiosity  
- OK with **Android** today (iOS later)  

---

## Screening question

For RD or for yourself in a CGM group:

> **“Will this person log meals and use a scale or CGM between visits?”**

| Answer | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Yes** | Candidate — offer MediLab |
| **No** | Not ICP — other tools or RD alone |

---

## Two channels (parallel, not either/or)

| Channel | Examples | What it proves |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| **Community / informal** | IL CGM WhatsApp/Facebook, friends, open beta APK, founder network | Strangers **stick**; word of mouth |
| **Clinic / RD** | Formal alpha site or casual “tell tracking clients” | **Trust** + RD workflow; B2B2C story |

Run **both** with the **same rule**.  
Example: 5 users from a group + 5 from one nutritionist = 10 honest tests.

---

## Who is **not** ICP (even if they see a nutritionist)

- Won’t log food between sessions  
- No devices, no intent to track  
- One visit for a static meal plan only  
- Casual “eat healthier” with no biomarker focus  
- Wrong clinical pathway for this app (pediatrics, ED, etc.)  

---

## Why this rule matters

| Without rule | With rule |
|--------------|-----------|
| Every nutritionist client = “market” | Only **trackers** enter funnel |
| High churn looks like product failure | Churn signal is **real** |
| Friend: “apps are enough” | You test **people who need fusion** |
| Marketing to billions | **Community + RD** find the same narrow user |

---

## One-sentence pitch (per channel)

**Community:**  
*“Libre + Withings + labs → daily macros in one app — if you log every day.”*

**Clinic / RD:**  
*“Your clients who track between visits — you get their full week before the session, not screenshots.”*
