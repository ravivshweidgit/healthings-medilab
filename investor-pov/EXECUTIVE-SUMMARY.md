**For:** investor / advisor review

## In one sentence

**MediLab closes the loop between CGM, food log, smart scale, and blood labs — and turns that into daily macro and meal decisions with human confirm, not black-box AI.**

The product is not a mockup. The founder:

- Uses the app **every day** as the primary metabolic decision system
- Achieved personal POC success (habit + outcomes — not pitching weight/LDL as the product proof)
- Shipped integrated features: CGM chat, `/macros`, `/recipe`, `/7` reviews, lab-aware macro engine, safety guards on auto-updates

**POC question answered:** *“Would I go back to Libre app + MFP + ChatGPT?”* → **No.**

## Israel market (external view)

Advisor estimate: **~500,000 relevant users in Israel, growing market.**

Rough framing (to validate, not audited):

- Rising **CGM** adoption (Libre and others), GLP-1 / cardiometabolic awareness
- **Hebrew-first** health UX is underserved vs US apps
- **Local lab formats** (e.g. Clalit-style chemistry panels) rarely integrated into daily nutrition tools
- Culturally strong interest in **food, family meals, and preventive health**

**Implication:** Even a **1–2%** penetration of a 500k SAM is **5,000–10,000 users** — enough for a **profitable niche business**, not necessarily a global unicorn.

| Scale | Users (1% of 500k) | At ~₪80/mo (~$22) | Annual |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------|--------|
| Conservative | 5,000 | — | ~$1.3M ARR |
| Moderate | 10,000 | — | ~$2.6M ARR |

*Illustrative only — assumes conversion and retention not yet proven.*

**Regional wedge:** MediLab already supports **Hebrew UI, local lab context, and bilingual commands** — hard for US-centric competitors to copy quickly.

## What we offer that others don’t (defensible niche)

Not “another CGM app” or “ChatGPT for health.”

| Others typically | MediLab |
|------------------|---------|
| CGM chart + generic tips | Same data feeds **meal decisions**, `/7` reviews, spike context |
| Food log + static calories | **Macro revision** from 7d burn + scale trend + **labs** |
| AI meal ideas | **Clinical-style rules** (lipids, kidney, glycemic) + **confirm before save** |
| One coach voice | **Coach / nutritionist / endocrinologist** on one dataset |
| Walled garden devices | **Bring-your-own:** Libre (HC/xDrip+), Withings, lab PDF |
| English-only | **Hebrew + English** |

**Positioning line:**

> *“Others sell dashboards. We sell decisions — daily targets and meals from your CGM, scale, labs, and log, with you in the loop.”*

## vs competitors (honest)

| Segment | Leaders | MediLab wins when… |
|---------|---------|-------------------|
| CGM lifestyle | Levels, Nutrisense, Signos | User wants **labs + macros + food loop**, not membership content |
| Food tracking | MFP, Cronometer | User wants **biomarker-driven targets**, not manual goals |
| Coaching | Noom | User wants **data-linked** advice, not behavior alone |
| Global scale / polish | Everyone | MediLab **loses** — we win **depth for configured power users** |

We do **not** compete on App Store polish or mass marketing today. We compete on **integration depth** for a **defined ICP**.

**ICP (hypothesis):** Adults with **CGM + smart scale**, focused on **lipids and/or glucose**, willing to log food; Israel first.
